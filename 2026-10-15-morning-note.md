**Current Price:** ~$5.84–$6.41 (range across sources Oct 13–14); **Daily Change:** -5.8% to -8.0% (Nasdaq POET down 5.8% Mon; OTC POETF down ~8% Tue). *Verify on live feed — stock is highly volatile.*

### News in Last 24 Hours
No new SEC filings or press releases confirmed for Oct 15. Most recent 6-K on record is dated August 14, 2026. Next scheduled earnings: **~November 12, 2026** (estimated). Monitor SEDAR+ and EDGAR for any new Form 6-K.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Ongoing.** Marvell (via Celestial AI acquisition) cancelled purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing POET's alleged breach of confidentiality obligations regarding the order and shipping disclosures. Stock fell 46–49% on disclosure. POET denies wrongdoing. No confirmed resolution or reinstatement of orders. Revenue impact unquantified. |
| **(2) Securities class actions (PFIC/tax misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Multiple firms (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton) filed class actions/probes alleging POET misrepresented its U.S. tax status (Passive Foreign Investment Company classification) and executive NDA breach. Allegations unproven; no settlement or dismissal reported. Class period and lead plaintiff status TBC. |
| **(3) $400M equity raise — dilution impact** | **Significant overhang.** POET sold ~19.05M shares + matching warrants at $21.00/unit in May 2026, raising ~$399.7M net. Warrants have 3-year term, exercise price ~$26.15–$26.25, with 9.99% beneficial ownership cap. Stock fell ~22% on announcement (~$16). At current ~$6 price, warrants are deep out of the money, but the share count expansion (~19M+ shares, potentially 38M+ fully diluted) remains a structural headwind. Proceeds earmarked for manufacturing scale (10x capacity by 2027), R&D, and M&A. |

### Outlook: **NEUTRAL-to-BEARISH (near-term)**

The stock is attempting to stabilize after catastrophic H1 2026 drawdowns (peak ~$20+ → trough ~$3.87), with reported bounces of 30%+ from the lows. However, the combination of unresolved litigation, a 22% dilutive raise whose proceeds remain undeployed at scale, the loss of the Marvell/Celestial AI relationship, and hot-CPI risk-off pressure on high-beta names creates a challenging setup heading into November earnings. A confirmed new large customer win or meaningful order update would be the catalyst to shift the view bullish.
