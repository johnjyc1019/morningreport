Gold is range-bound, pressured by the strong dollar and rising yields.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.41–7.42 | **Daily Change:** −3.83% (Oct 7 close) | **Market Cap:** ~$1.28B | **52-Wk Range:** $3.87 – $20.81

**Cash position:** ~$796M (post-financing). No new filings or press releases detected for October 8, 2026.

### Key Risk Status

**(1) Marvell / Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit**
*Active risk.* On April 27, 2026, Marvell Semiconductor cancelled all Celestial AI purchase orders, citing POET's alleged disclosure of confidential order/shipping data in breach of NDA. Stock fell ~47% on the announcement. POET has noted a separate ~$5M purchase order from another technology customer and says its roadmap is intact. No update found today on dispute resolution or litigation by either party.

**(2) Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**
*Active risk; lead-plaintiff stage likely concluded.* Multiple class action suits (lead case: *Jones v. POET Technologies*, D.N.J. No. 26-cv-04717) allege POET misrepresented its Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) status under U.S. tax law, that the CFO breached an NDA in a public interview, and that executives signed false SOX certifications. The lead-plaintiff deadline of June 29, 2026 has passed; the case is likely in early discovery or motion practice. POET announced plans to redomicile in the U.S. and publish QEF election materials following Wolfpack Research's April 14 short report, which precipitated an 8% single-day drop.

**(3) $400M Raise Dilution Impact**
*Ongoing overhang.* POET closed a $400M registered direct offering on May 18, 2026 at $21/share (19.05M shares + equal-number warrants at ~$26.15–26.25/share strike, 3-year term). Stock fell ~22% on announcement. At ~$7.41 today, the raise was done at nearly 3× the current price, and warrants are deeply out-of-the-money. Potential dilution from warrants is ~12% of current share count if exercised, but exercise is unlikely at current prices. The capital is being deployed toward a targeted 10× wafer production expansion by 2027 and potential acquisitions.

### Outlook: **Bearish / High Risk**

POET trades at ~64% below the May offering price with three overlapping overhangs (litigation, dilution, lost anchor customer). While the $796M cash position provides runway and the manufacturing buildout thesis is intact, near-term catalysts are absent, and legal clarity will likely take quarters. The stock is range-trading between its 52-week low (~$3.87) and the ~$10–12 resistance band. Avoid adding until litigation provides clearer resolution or a new material customer win is disclosed.
