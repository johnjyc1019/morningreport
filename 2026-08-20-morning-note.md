# Morning Note — August 20, 2026

## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures are muted to modestly lower in premarket, with S&P 500 and Dow futures near flat and Nasdaq 100 futures off ~0.5%. Technology names — Oracle, AMD, Micron, and Marvell — are all tracking lower, extending the prior session's tech weakness. The broader tone reflects three compounding headwinds: persistent inflation, a hawkish-leaning Fed, and a geopolitical flashpoint in the Strait of Hormuz.

July CPI came in at +0.1% MoM / +3.4% YoY — in-line with consensus but still elevated enough to keep the Fed cautious. The July FOMC minutes revealed three dissenting members who favored a rate hike, though a weak July jobs report has tempered September rate-hike odds. The Fed funds rate stands at 3.50%–3.75%. The 10-year Treasury yield retreated to **4.65%** after touching a near-20-month high of 4.75%, as Treasury announced expanded buybacks on long-dated securities.

Oil is front-of-mind today: a 60-day US-Iran ceasefire expires August 20, with no deal in sight. Senior Iranian officials have signaled possible escalation in the Strait of Hormuz, through which ~20% of global oil supply transits.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Short Nikkei / Long USD-JPY hedge** — BoJ rate-hike fears, rising oil (terms of trade hit for Japan), and weak GDP combine for continued Nikkei pressure. BoJ meeting risk is elevated.

2. **Energy longs (XLE / CVX / SLB)** — Iran ceasefire expiry on Aug 20 with no deal creates binary oil-spike risk. A Hormuz escalation would reprice oil sharply higher. Risk/reward favors upside calls on near-term crude.

3. **Fade memory chip relief rallies** — Micron, SK Hynix, and Sandisk are down 4%+ on macro concerns. Until the macro picture clears (Fed, yields, AI capex cycle), semiconductor memory remains a sell-the-rip.

4. **Home Depot (HD) neutral** — The double beat with flat guidance is a dead cat. Housing market headwinds and unchanged outlook cap upside. Hold for now, revisit on guidance revision.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$8.48 (as of Aug 18, 2026) | **52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81

### Recent News (Last 24–48 Hours)
- No major new filings or press releases in the past 24 hours as of time of writing.
- Board appointment of Dr. Bardia Pezeshki (optical comms expert, ex-Avicena/Kaiam/Santur) and Jean F. Rankin (legal/governance) effective August 1 continues to be the most recent positive catalyst.
- Production ramp for optical engines remains on schedule for H2 2026; capacity target: ~1M units/month by end of 2027.

### Key Risk Status

1. **Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit**
   — ACTIVE RISK. Marvell cancelled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing confidentiality breaches; stock fell 46% on April 27. The NDA violation allegation (POET CFO allegedly disclosed deal terms publicly) remains central to ongoing securities litigation. No settlement or resolution has been announced.

2. **Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**
   — ACTIVE RISK, PAST DEADLINE. Multiple law firms (Faruqi & Faruqi, Kirby McInerney, others) filed securities class actions covering the April 1–27, 2026 class period. The lead-plaintiff deadline of June 29, 2026 has passed; litigation is now proceeding. POET allegedly concealed that it "likely" qualified as a PFIC under US tax law — a material adverse tax consequence for US shareholders.

3. **$400M Raise Dilution Impact**
   — PARTIALLY DIGESTED. POET closed a $400M registered direct offering on May 18, 2026 at $21.00/share (19.05M shares + matching warrants at $26.25 exercise price); the stock fell 22% that day to ~$15.97 and has since declined further to ~$8.48 — well below the offering price. The company has raised $830M in equity over the past 12 months and may raise up to $661M more via outstanding warrants.

### Outlook: **BEARISH / HIGH RISK**

The stock is trading ~60% below the May capital raise price and ~59% below the 52-week high, with active securities litigation, unresolved NDA/order-cancellation disputes with Marvell, and a diluted float. Near-term catalysts are limited absent production ramp evidence; retail interest (based on recent price action from ~$6.36 to $8.59 by Aug 6) may provide temporary support, but fundamentals and legal overhangs favor continued pressure.
