## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price:** $9.70 | **Daily Change:** −0.20% | **Market Cap:** ~$1.14B

### Last 24 Hours
No material new press releases or SEC filings detected in the last 24 hours. Stock has stabilized in the $9–10 range following months of elevated volatility.

### Key Risk Status

**1. Marvell / Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit**
*Status: Active litigation risk.* On April 23, 2026, Marvell Semiconductor provided written notice cancelling all purchase orders tied to Celestial AI, citing POET's alleged breach of confidentiality obligations (a senior executive had publicly discussed invoice/shipping timelines in a video interview). The stock collapsed 47.3% (−$7.15/share) on the April 27 disclosure. The NDA breach is now a central element of shareholder class action complaints. Resolution timeline unknown; discovery likely ongoing.

**2. Securities Class Action Lawsuits (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**
*Status: Active; lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026.* Multiple law firms (Faruqi & Faruqi, Rosen Law Firm, Block & Leviton) filed or launched class actions covering shareholders between approximately April 1 – April 27, 2026. Core allegations: (a) POET's 2025 Annual Report understated the likelihood of PFIC classification under U.S. tax law using equivocal language ("may be treated"), and (b) an executive's public disclosure breached the Marvell NDA. The lead plaintiff appointment process is complete as of the June 29 deadline; the consolidated case is likely in early motion-to-dismiss phase.

**3. $400M Capital Raise — Dilution Impact**
*Status: Completed; dilution material.* POET closed a registered direct offering of 19,047,620 common shares plus 19,047,620 warrants (combined price $21.00/share+warrant) for gross proceeds of $400M from a single institutional investor. Full warrant exercise would bring total new shares to ~38.1M. The stock fell over 22% on the close date. Proceeds earmarked for manufacturing scale-up, acquisitions, and R&D. Given the stock is now at $9.70 vs. the $21 raise price, the institutional buyer is significantly underwater; warrant exercise is unlikely at current levels, limiting secondary dilution near-term.

### Outlook: **BEARISH / NEUTRAL**
The three-pronged overhang — order cancellation, securities litigation, and dilutive raise far above current prices — leaves no obvious near-term catalyst to re-rate the stock higher. Until the class actions reach a settlement or dismissal, and until POET can announce a replacement customer for the Marvell/Celestial AI revenue hole, the risk/reward remains unfavorable. The $9–10 zone may offer technical support, but any litigation development or secondary selling from the institutional investor could break it.
