## Macro Backdrop

Markets enter Tuesday on a cautious footing after Monday's broad tech-led selloff. The S&P 500 shed 0.5% and the Nasdaq 100 fell 0.8%, with the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index cratering 5.9% (Nvidia -3.4%, Intel -5.6%) as a wave of AI development concern swept through markets. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called publicly for a slower pace of advanced AI development; the view was endorsed by OpenAI's Sam Altman and xAI's Elon Musk. President Trump pushed back, lashing out at Amodei directly.

The bond market remains the dominant macro story. The 10-year Treasury yield briefly touched the psychologically significant **5.00%** level before pulling back to close at **4.99%**, its highest since October 2023. August CPI printed in line at ~**3.4%** headline — sticky, well above the Fed's 2% target, and consistent with a five-year streak of above-target inflation. CME FedWatch now shows **~92% probability** of a 25bps hike at this week's FOMC meeting. Oil is adding to the inflationary pressure: Brent surged on Middle East tensions after planned Iran-Gulf diplomatic talks collapsed.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Short semis via SOXS / put spreads on SOXX** — The Philly Semi Index -5.9% Monday signals a regime shift in AI sentiment, not just a one-day flush. If FOMC hikes 25bps this week and rates stay elevated, growth multiples compress further. Risk: any positive AI/demand news could trigger violent squeeze.

2. **Long energy (XLE, OXY) on geopolitical/supply premium** — Brent at $108 with Middle East diplomacy stalled and WTI back above $100 creates a durable tailwind. Watch for OPEC commentary mid-week.

3. **Avoid discretionary (XRT, DBRSX)** — Dave & Buster's and Children's Place earnings confirm the consumer spending cracks telegraphed in recent macro data. High rates + sticky inflation = continued pressure on lower-income cohort.

4. **Short-dated UST puts / TBT exposure** — If FOMC hikes and signals "higher for longer," the 10-year could decisively breach 5.00% and reprice toward 5.25%+. Tail risk is a dovish surprise, but at 92% hike odds, market is priced in.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price (as of Sept 14 close area): ~$7.40–$7.50 | Est. daily change: -1% to -2%**
*(Sept 13 close: $7.95; Sept 14 intraday range: $7.38–$7.54)*

### Last 24 Hours
No material new filings or press releases identified for September 14–15. The stock remains in a consolidation range following its catastrophic April 2026 collapse. POET exhibited at the China International Optoelectronic Expo (CIOE) September 9–11, highlighting wafer-level chip-scale packaging and AI data center connectivity solutions. Q2 2026 revenue was reported at $569,925, up 112% YoY — representing a sixth consecutive quarter of sequential growth from a very small base.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|------|--------|
| **Marvell / Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | **ACTIVE / ONGOING.** On April 23, 2026, Marvell sent written notice of NDA breach by a senior POET executive; by April 27, all Celestial AI purchase orders were cancelled. Stock fell 47.3% on the day. NDA-related litigation is ongoing with no announced resolution. |
| **Securities Class Actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **IN ACTIVE LITIGATION.** Class action filed April 28, 2026 in U.S. District Court (D.N.J.) alleging POET and executives made materially false and misleading statements regarding PFIC (passive foreign investment company) tax status, negative U.S. tax implications for investors, overstated valuation, and the NDA breach. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 — case is now past that threshold and in active discovery/litigation phase. Multiple law firms (incl. Rosen Law) are involved. |
| **$400M Capital Raise Dilution Impact** | **SIGNIFICANT / ONGOING OVERHANG.** On May 15, 2026, POET raised $400M in a registered direct offering: 19,047,620 common shares + equal number of warrants, priced at **$21.00/share** (warrant exercise price: $26.15, 3-year term). The offering closed May 18. With the stock now at ~$7.40–7.50, both the offering price and warrant strike are **deeply out of the money** — representing ~65% underwater on the offer price. While the raise provided a large cash cushion for manufacturing scale-up, R&D, and acquisitions, the massive dilution (19M+ new shares) at a price now far above market is a persistent overhang and reflects how sharply the stock has declined since the raise. |

### Outlook: **BEARISH**
POET's stock has declined roughly 50%+ since its pre-Marvell/Celestial AI cancellation levels and is trading well below its May capital raise price. The fundamental revenue base remains tiny (~$570K/quarter) despite impressive growth percentages. Legal exposure from both the NDA lawsuit and securities class actions is unresolved and material. The $400M raise, while providing liquidity, significantly diluted shareholders at a price now far above market, leaving the stock with minimal near-term technical support. Until there is a clear resolution to litigation and evidence of meaningful revenue scale, the risk/reward is asymmetric to the downside.
