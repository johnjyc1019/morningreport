## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$4.46–4.59 (Nov 17 close: $4.40; Nov 18 intraday: $4.37–$4.92)
**Daily % Change:** Modest decline; Benzinga (Nov 18–19) flagged continued selling: *"POET Stock Continues to Drop"*
**52-week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81 | **Market Cap:** ~$760–766M
**Shares Outstanding:** ~174M (post-raise)

### News in Last 24 Hours
- **Q3 2026 Results (filed Nov 16):** Net loss narrowed to $7.56M from $9.37M YoY; NRE (non-recurring engineering) revenue grew to $1.03M from $0.30M—small but directionally positive. No product revenue.
- **New CFO:** Giovanni M. Pacelli took over November 2, 2026; predecessor Thomas Mika departing December 2. POET to begin hosting quarterly earnings calls starting with Q4 2026 results.
- **CoreWeave catalyst (prior week):** POET spiked +6% on ~Nov 3 and again on CoreWeave's strong Q3 results (~Nov 10–11); gains have since fully reversed.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Active. Marvell (post-Celestial AI acquisition) cancelled all purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing POET's breach of confidentiality obligations regarding order/shipping disclosures. POET disputes the rationale; legal proceedings ongoing. All backlog from that relationship is gone. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC misrepresentation)** | Active. Multiple class actions filed over the April 1–27, 2026 window. Plaintiffs allege POET misrepresented its US tax status as a likely Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC), and that an executive breached an NDA in a public setting. Lead-plaintiff motion deadline was reportedly June 29, 2026—cases likely consolidated. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | Severe. Raise closed May 2026: ~19M shares at ~$21/share + warrants for another ~19M shares. Stock dropped 22% on announcement day to $15.97 and has since fallen to ~$4.46—a 72% decline from the pre-raise area. Dilution approximately 21% at issuance, before warrant exercise. |

### Brief Outlook: **Bearish / High-Risk Speculative**

POET is trading near 52-week lows with no product revenue, a cancelled flagship customer relationship, multiple active securities lawsuits, and massive post-raise dilution weighing on the stock. The only near-term bullish catalyst is an AI photonics re-rating if a credible new customer announces orders—absent that, the path of least resistance is lower toward the $3.87 support. Only suitable for high-risk-tolerant speculators.
