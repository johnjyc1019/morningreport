## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price (Nov 17 close):** ~$4.43 | **Daily change:** –4.94%  
**52-week range:** $3.87 (low) – $20.81 (high)  
**Market cap:** ~$891M (pre-Q3 close; likely reduced further at current price)

### Latest News (last 24 hours)
POET reported Q3 results on November 16, triggering a two-day selloff. Q3 revenue came in at **$1.03M (+244% YoY)** — a record — but missed the $1.60M consensus estimate. EPS beat: loss of **$0.04** vs. a –$0.07 consensus. CEO Suresh Venkatesan reiterated a target of **10x manufacturing capacity expansion** heading into 2027, funded by the May 2026 $400M raise. No new SEC filings or press releases detected in the last 24 hours beyond the Q3 report.

### Key Risk Tracker

1. **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit:**  
   On April 23, 2026, Marvell (which had acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all POET purchase orders, citing a breach of POET's confidentiality obligations after CFO Thomas Mika disclosed the existence of the order on Stocktwits on April 21. POET shares fell 47%+ on the April 27 announcement. No resolution disclosed; company says it is fulfilling orders with other customers including a ~$5M order. *Status: Ongoing — material overhang.*

2. **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation):**  
   Multiple class actions filed April 28–May 2026 (Rosen Law, Kirby McInerney, Gibbs Mura, SueWallSt) covering a class period of April 1–27, 2026. Allegations: POET misrepresented its passive foreign investment company (PFIC) status under US tax law. POET confirmed it was classified as a PFIC for fiscal year 2025 in subsequent 6-K filings. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 (passed). *Status: Litigation likely in discovery phase — no reported settlement.*

3. **$400M capital raise dilution impact:**  
   On May 14–15, 2026, POET raised ~$399.7M via a direct offering of 19,047,620 shares + warrants at $21.00/unit to a single institutional investor. This represented ~21% immediate dilution; stock fell 22% on the announcement to $15.97. Warrants are exercisable at ~$26.15–$26.25 for three years. CEO framed the raise as funding for 10x manufacturing scale-up. *Status: Raise closed; dilution baked in; warrants remain a secondary overhang if stock recovers above strike.*

### Outlook: **Bearish / Cautious**
POET is in a structurally weak technical position — trading near its 52-week low at $4.43, down ~79% from the post-raise highs, with a revenue miss on Q3 compounding investor frustration. Three concurrent legal/regulatory overhangs (NDA cancellation, PFIC class actions, dilution fears) are suppressing institutional buyers. Near-term catalysts (new PO announcements, manufacturing progress update) could spark relief rallies, but absent a material contract win or legal resolution, risk/reward skews negative. Best case for longs is a recovery toward $6–7 on new customer announcements; worst case is continued drift toward the $3.87 52-week low.
