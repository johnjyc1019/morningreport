## Macro Backdrop

Risk sentiment remains fragile heading into Wednesday's session. The dominant narrative is a three-way collision: AI/chip supply uncertainty after a Chinese memory breakthrough claim drove a broad tech rout Tuesday; elevated rates with the 10-year Treasury holding near **5.22–5.28%** (highest since 2002); and geopolitical pressure from renewed Iran-related disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz pushing Brent above **$106** and WTI near **$95**.

The Fed held its September 16 FOMC meeting, delivering a 12-0 hike that brought the target range to **3.75%–4.00%**. The dot plot signals one more hike in 2026 (now re-priced to December after the weak jobs print). NY Fed President Williams reiterated that posture Tuesday, saying no further tightening is needed in 2027. Markets are focused on **September CPI**, due later this week, as the swing factor for December's decision — August headline CPI printed at **3.4% YoY**, core at **2.4%**.

The **dollar is firmer** while **gold has corrected** on yield pressure. The JPY caught a bid overnight after a BoJ source report suggested possible policy adjustment. IMF/World Bank Annual Meetings run through October 18 in Bangkok; G20 finance ministers meet tomorrow (October 15).

## Trade Ideas

1. **Long GS, cautious JPM:** Goldman's blowout beat vs JPM's miss underscores business-mix matters in this rate environment. Trading and advisory revenues at Goldman holding up; JPM's net interest income more rate-sensitive. Risk: macro deterioration compresses multiples.

2. **Watch ASML as AI demand indicator:** If ASML raises guidance again (has done so twice already this year), it validates continued data-center capex and is bullish for the broader chip equipment chain (AMAT, KLAC, LRCX). If cautious, confirms AI spending is bifurcating.

3. **Energy stay long, oil above $100:** Brent >$106 with Strait of Hormuz disruptions persistent. XLE and European integrated majors (Shell, BP) offer relative value vs US small-cap exploration names.

4. **Fade NQ bounces near-term:** Until the CPI print and clarity on the Chinese chip claim, the path of least resistance for Nasdaq is lower. AI valuation multiples are especially vulnerable to rate persistence at 5%+.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price:** ~$6.32 (as of October 13 close) | **Daily Change:** -5.8% on October 12 (from ~$6.97)
**52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.09B

### News (Last 24 Hours)
No major new press releases or SEC filings identified for October 14. Stock continues to trade below its $21.00 offering price from May and has partially recovered from the April lows (~$3.87). MarketBeat consensus: **Sell** with $8.00 price target.

### Key Risk Status

**1. Marvell / Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Dispute**
On April 23, 2026, Marvell (which acquired Celestial AI in February 2026) cancelled all outstanding POET purchase orders, citing alleged confidentiality breaches — POET had publicly disclosed order and shipping details without Marvell's authorization. POET's stock plunged ~46% on April 27 disclosure. Company responded by pointing to a separate ~$5M order from an unnamed technology customer as evidence of continued commercial traction. No formal NDA lawsuit has been reported (the dispute remains a contract-cancellation matter). *Status: Unresolved; remains an overhang on any new customer announcement credibility.*

**2. Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**
Multiple firms (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, Kirby McInerney) filed class actions in the U.S. District Court for the District of New Jersey covering the April 1–27, 2026 class period. Core allegations: (a) POET misrepresented its Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) tax status — which could impose punitive US tax treatment on shareholders — until a Wolfpack Research report on April 14 forced disclosure; (b) CFO Thomas Mika allegedly violated confidentiality obligations by publicly discussing business arrangements. SOX certification fraud also alleged. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026. *Status: Active litigation; no settlement or dismissal reported. Unknown current case posture.*

**3. $400M Raise Dilution Impact**
In May 2026, POET closed a registered direct offering of 19,047,620 shares + warrants at $21.00/unit, raising ~$399.7M net. Warrants exercisable at $26.15 for three years (capped at 9.99% beneficial ownership). Stock fell ~22% on announcement (May 15 close ~$15.97). Q2 2026 cash and short-term investments: $796.3M. Q2 net loss narrowed to $11.3M from $12.3M (Q1). Proceeds earmarked for manufacturing expansion, acquisitions, and R&D. *Status: Fully dilutive if warrants are exercised; balance sheet now well-funded but share count impact is significant.*

### Outlook: **Neutral / Cautiously Bearish**
POET has rebuilt its cash cushion significantly and narrowed losses, but three open overhangs — the Marvell dispute's chilling effect on future customer announcements, active securities litigation, and ~$21 offering price vs. ~$6 current price implying the institutional buyer is deeply underwater — suggest limited near-term catalyst for sustained upside without new commercial orders from credible, named customers.
