**Current Price:** ~$8.37 (Sept 9, 2026 close) | **52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.44B

### Latest News (Last 24 Hours)
- Q2 2026 results previously reported: revenue **+112% YoY**, sixth consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth. CEO reaffirmed 2026 production ramp is "on schedule." Cash position improved substantially following the $400M raise.
- No material new SEC filings or press releases identified in the last 24 hours as of publication.

### Key Risk Tracker

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** On April 23, 2026, Marvell (acquirer of Celestial AI) canceled all POET purchase orders, citing confidentiality breaches by CFO Thomas Mika who discussed shipment/invoice details in a public YouTube interview. Stock fell **47.3%** on April 27. The NDA breach is a core allegation in ongoing securities class actions. No settlement or resolution announced. |
| **(2) Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Multiple firms (Rosen Law Firm, Block & Leviton, Faruqi & Faruqi, others) filed class actions covering the period April 1 – April 27, 2026. Allegations: (a) POET misrepresented its passive foreign investment company (PFIC) status under US tax law, creating undisclosed negative tax consequences for US shareholders; (b) CFO NDA breach. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 — cases likely now in consolidation/discovery phase. Material litigation overhang. |
| **(3) $400M Raise Dilution Impact** | **Ongoing Overhang.** POET raised $400M via registered direct offering at $21.00/share (19.05M new shares). Stock plunged 22% on deal close to ~$15.97. An additional 19.05M warrants are exercisable at $26.15 for 3 years — potential additional ~30%+ dilution at current prices. Proceeds directed at 10x manufacturing capacity expansion and R&D. Strategic intent is sound but near-term EPS dilution is substantial. |

### Outlook: **NEUTRAL / CAUTIOUS BEARISH**
The Q2 revenue acceleration (+112% YoY) and production ramp updates are genuinely positive, but the stock is encumbered by active securities litigation, a meaningful warrant overhang from the $400M raise, and lingering uncertainty about whether POET can replace the lost Marvell/Celestial AI pipeline with comparable revenue. Until at least one of the three major risk factors above reaches resolution, the risk/reward remains skewed to the downside. Watch for any new customer announcements or litigation developments as potential re-rating catalysts.
