## Macro Backdrop

September CPI came in hot at **3.7% YoY** (core: 2.6%), pushing the 10-year Treasury yield near **5.08–5.09%** — not far off the 24-year intraday high of 5.37% hit in early October. Energy remains the headline driver: Brent is trading near **$110/bbl** and WTI near **$101/bbl**, supported by tanker attacks threatening Persian Gulf supply and Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil infrastructure. Oil briefly dipped on reports Trump floated partial Russia sanctions relief, but no final decision has been made. US equities are staging a modest recovery this morning after last week's tech-led rout (Nasdaq -2.5% on Friday).

## Trade Ideas

1. **Defense / Aerospace (Long RTX, GE, LMT):** Geopolitical risk premium is real — Ukraine escalation, Strait of Hormuz disruption, and elevated defense budgets globally. RTX and GE earnings today could act as near-term catalysts.

2. **Energy (Long XOM, CVX via short-dated calls):** Brent above $110 with supply-side risk intact. Any further escalation in the Gulf or fresh sanction uncertainty provides upside. Risk: a Russia deal or demand destruction from high rates.

3. **Avoid high-multiple tech into FOMC (Oct 27–28):** With the 10-year near 5.1% and hike odds still live, valuations on unprofitable or AI-hype names remain at risk. The Nasdaq bounce this morning looks tactical, not structural.

4. **Netflix (NFLX) — event play:** Into tonight's print, the market is pricing a ~7.3% swing. The stock has fallen after 5 of the last 6 reports despite EPS beats; if guidance disappoints, consider puts on any premarket strength.
