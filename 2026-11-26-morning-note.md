## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$3.24–$3.46 (Nasdaq) | **Daily Change:** -3% to -9% (Wednesday, Nov 25)
*US markets closed today; next live print on Friday Nov 27.*

POET now trades **below its own cash pile** (~$774M raised at $21/share in May 2026), with market cap near ~$596M. The stock is within striking distance of its 52-week low of $3.36.

---

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** On April 23, 2026, Marvell (which had acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all purchase orders, citing POET CFO Thomas Mika's April 21 Stocktwits disclosure of the Celestial AI order as a breach of their NDA. The cancellation triggered a 45%+ intraday stock collapse on April 27. POET has not publicly disclosed whether it is pursuing arbitration or legal action against Marvell. The company cited a separate ~$5M purchase order with another unnamed tech firm as an ongoing customer relationship. |
| **Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** | **Filed / Lead Plaintiff Deadline Passed.** Case no. 3:26-cv-04717 (D.N.J.). Class period: April 1–27, 2026. Allegations: (1) CFO's Stocktwits disclosure triggered NDA breach and was materially misleading; (2) POET understated the probability of PFIC classification — a tax status that creates punitive U.S. shareholder consequences. POET has since confirmed it was classified as a PFIC for fiscal 2025 but believes it will not qualify for 2026. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026. Case is now in discovery/litigation phase. |
| **$400M Capital Raise Dilution** | **Structural Overhang.** May 2026 registered direct offering: 19,047,620 shares + warrants (19,047,620 shares, exercisable at $26.15, 3-year term) to a single institutional investor at $21/unit. Immediate dilution of ~21% on existing shares. Warrants represent a further ~17% overhang on current share count if exercised. Stock fell 22% the day of announcement. Capital was earmarked for manufacturing scale-up and the Lumilens AI photonics deal. |

---

### Outlook: **BEARISH (Near-Term)**

POET's fundamentals remain speculative — Q3 2026 revenue of $1.03M missed the $1.60M consensus by 36% — and the technical picture is deteriorating alongside the broader AI sell-off. At current prices, the stock is essentially a bet on management's ability to convert its $774M cash balance and AI-photonics technology into commercial scale before litigation costs and dilution fears erode investor confidence further. The loss of the Marvell/Celestial AI relationship, which was the company's flagship design win, removes the clearest near-term revenue catalyst. Without a new tier-1 customer announcement or a favorable lawsuit development, the path of least resistance is lower.
