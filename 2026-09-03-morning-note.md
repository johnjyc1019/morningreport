## Macro Backdrop

Markets are navigating a confluence of risks as September kicks off: US-Iran military hostilities have driven Brent crude to ~$95/bbl, a six-week high, stoking inflation fears and keeping central banks on edge. Wednesday's ADP print came in at just 38K private payrolls added in August — well below the 47K consensus and the softest reading since January — raising doubts about Friday's NFP and dampening near-term Fed rate-hike expectations. The 10-year Treasury yield settled little changed at **4.79%** after the soft ADP data, while New York Fed President Williams struck a dovish note, stating inflation "continues to trend down." Stocks managed modest gains Wednesday on those signals, but crude-driven inflation anxiety remains the dominant headwind.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Energy longs (XLE / CVX / SLB)** — US-Iran hostilities pushing Brent above $95; risk premium not fully priced in integrated majors. Catalyst: further Strait of Hormuz escalation or supply disruption.

2. **Cybersecurity (PANW / CRWD)** — PANW's strong quarter reaffirms enterprise demand durability; sector remains a secular spend priority. Consider on any broad market pullback.

3. **Fade rate-sensitive growth on NFP risk** — Soft ADP creates a binary: a weak NFP Friday would confirm slowdown and may actually lift equities (fewer hikes); a strong print would re-ignite yield pressure. Keep duration exposure hedged into Friday.

4. **Short RH / discretionary names with tariff exposure** — RH's miss underscores that tariff costs are flowing through to margins. Sector weakness likely to persist if trade policy remains restrictive.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.30 | **Session Range (Sep 2):** $6.96 – $7.30

*POET has declined ~65% from its $21.00 registered-direct offering price in June 2026 and ~85%+ from pre-cancellation highs.*

### News in Last 24 Hours
- No material press releases or SEC filings detected for September 2–3, 2026 (routine monitoring ongoing).
- Most recent filings: Registration statement and Q2 2026 earnings both released **August 13, 2026**.
- Q2 results provided limited revenue visibility given the loss of the Marvell/Celestial AI relationship; investors focused on burn rate and the Lumilens order ($50M initial, up to $500M over 5 years) as the primary revenue replacement thesis.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | Active — Marvell cancelled all purchase orders April 23, 2026, citing confidentiality breaches (CEO disclosed deal details in a YouTube interview April 21). Stock fell 47% on April 27 announcement. Litigation exposure unresolved. |
| **Securities Class Actions (PFIC / securities fraud)** | Active — Lead plaintiff deadline passed June 29, 2026. Multiple firms filed (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, KTMC, Faruqi & Faruqi, DJS Law Group). Allegations cover PFIC tax misrepresentation and securities fraud surrounding the order cancellation disclosures. Cases in discovery/early litigation phase. |
| **$400M Capital Raise Dilution** | Closed — 19,047,620 shares issued at $21.00 in registered direct offering; stock fell 22.4% to $15.97 on closing day (late May/June 2026). Warrants exercisable at $26.15 for 3 years could add another 19M shares. At current ~$7.30 price, warrants are deeply out of the money, reducing near-term dilution risk, but the capital raise underscores management's need for external funding at a price now far above market. |

### Outlook: **Bearish**
The stock has continued to bleed lower post-raise, now trading at ~35% of the offering price. With no confirmed revenue-generating customer to replace Marvell/Celestial AI at scale, multiple active lawsuits, and ongoing cash burn, the risk/reward remains unfavorable. The Lumilens relationship is the sole near-term fundamental positive, but at $7.30 the market is pricing in substantial execution and litigation risk. No near-term catalyst visible to reverse trend absent a major new customer announcement or lawsuit resolution.
