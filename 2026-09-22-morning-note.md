## Macro Backdrop

US equities closed strongly on Monday, with the S&P 500 rising **+1.49% to 7,764.70** and the Nasdaq Composite surging **+2.26% to a record close of 27,122.09** — both indices' best sessions since early August, driven by a rebound in AI-related names and a pullback in oil and Treasury yields. The Dow added 366 points (+0.71%) to 52,048.83.

Overnight futures are little changed: S&P futures +0.09%, Nasdaq-100 futures +0.05%, Dow futures +28 pts (+0.05%).

The macro backdrop remains complicated. The **Federal Reserve hiked rates 25 bps** for the first time in three years at last week's meeting (September 16–17), bringing the Fed Funds target to **3.75%–4.00%**. The dot plot signals one additional hike — most likely December, with October skipped given proximity to midterm elections. August CPI came in line with expectations but remains well above the 2% target. The **10-year Treasury yield** climbed back to **~5.00%** post-hike before pulling back modestly, pressured by falling oil.

**Brent crude** fell ~3.67% to ~$100.06/bbl on Monday — still elevated due to the ongoing Iran conflict, but below recent highs. WTI followed lower.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.80 | **Daily Change (Mon Sep 21):** +10.51%

### 24-Hour News

- **China AI Tailwind:** POET shares surged +10.51% Monday driven by headlines around China's aggressive AI data center buildout gaining steam — POET's optical engine technology is positioned as a key enabler for hyperscale connectivity in Chinese AI infrastructure. The company recently exhibited at the 2026 China International Optoelectronic Expo (CIOE, September 9–11).
- **$150M Registered Direct Offering:** Seeking Alpha reports POET priced a $150M registered direct offering of shares — this is an additional dilutive raise following the June 2026 $400M offering. Terms/pricing not fully confirmed at time of writing; represents continued access to capital markets but accelerates dilution overhang.
- **Q2 2026 Revenue:** Unaudited Q2 revenue of $569,925 (+13% QoQ, +112% YoY) — sixth consecutive quarter of sequential growth; net loss narrowed to $11.3M. $50M initial order from Lumilens noted as a positive development.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | Marvell canceled all POET purchase orders on April 27, 2026, citing NDA/confidentiality breaches (a company executive allegedly disclosed Celestial AI relationship details in a YouTube video). Stock fell 47.3% on disclosure. Legal proceedings ongoing — no settlement announced. Remains a major unresolved overhang. |
| **(2) Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** | Multiple class actions filed (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, SueWallSt.com) for the April 1–27, 2026 class period, alleging POET understated the likelihood of being classified as a Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) for U.S. tax purposes. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026; cases now proceeding. POET confirmed PFIC status for year ended December 31, 2025. Litigation adds legal cost burden and reputational risk. |
| **(3) $400M Raise Dilution Impact (+ $150M Follow-On)** | June 2026: 19.05M shares + warrants issued at $21.00 (a slim premium to then-market price of $20.57); stock fell 22% on closing day. Since then stock has declined ~63% from offer price to ~$7.80. The additional $150M raise implies further share issuance at current depressed levels, deepening dilution for existing holders. Full warrant exercise would add another 19M shares. |

### Outlook: **Neutral / Cautiously Bearish**

The +10.51% Monday pop on China AI data center headlines is a positive near-term catalyst and shows the stock can move sharply on sentiment, but the fundamental picture remains complicated: three unresolved overhangs (litigation, NDA dispute, serial dilution), sub-$600K quarterly revenue, and an ~$11M/quarter cash burn that necessitates continued equity raises. The $150M follow-on at prices far below the June $400M raise signals management is still consuming capital well ahead of commercial scale. Hold/watch rather than new long unless there is a definitive resolution on the Marvell dispute or a transformative new customer order.
