## Macro Backdrop

Yesterday's July CPI print came in tame at **+0.1% MoM / +3.4% YoY** (vs. 3.5% prior), matching consensus and extending the market-friendly disinflation narrative. Core CPI also met estimates. The softer reading fueled a broad risk-on rally in equities, led by AI and tech. However, the Fed remains cautious — Chicago Fed's Austan Goolsbee reiterated officials are more focused on inflation persistence than labor market weakness, and roughly half of FOMC members have penciled in a rate hike this year. The **10-year Treasury yield** eased to **4.68%** after briefly touching 4.71%, as diplomatic signals of a potential U.S.-Iran deal tempered energy and inflation fears. **WTI crude** is at **$84.61/bbl**; **Brent** had surged to ~$100.69 earlier this month on geopolitical tensions but has since pulled back slightly. Trade policy remains a headwind: the Trump administration announced a new tariff round — 50% on select Canadian goods, 25% on Brazil, and 10–12% on ~60 additional trading partners.

### Recent News (Last 24–48 Hours)
- No major new filings or press releases as of Aug 13 morning.
- **Aug 4–6:** POET refreshed its Board of Directors, appointing Dr. Bardia Pezeshki and Jean F. Rankin; long-serving director Jean-Louis Malinge resigned for personal reasons. Board additions signal deepened focus on AI data center optics commercialization.
- **Aug 12:** 247 Wall St. piece highlighted ~147% analyst upside target following the $400M capital raise; investor focus shifting toward revenue execution milestones.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Marvell cancelled all Celestial AI purchase orders on Apr 23, 2026, citing POET's alleged confidentiality breach (an executive discussed order/shipping details publicly on YouTube). Stock fell 47% on disclosure. This risk is now a known overhang; the cancellation is complete. Litigation outcome uncertain. **Ongoing.** |
| **(2) Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | Multiple class actions filed (Rosen, Block & Leviton, Faruqi & Faruqi) over period Apr 1–27, 2026. Core allegation: POET misrepresented its PFIC tax status for U.S. shareholders and downplayed likelihood of classification. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 — cases now in litigation phase. **Material, ongoing legal risk.** |
| **(3) $400M raise dilution impact** | POET closed a registered direct offering raising US$400M from a single institutional investor: 19,047,620 common shares + warrant for equivalent shares at $21.00/unit. This represents meaningful dilution (~10–15% of float depending on starting count) and warrant overhang at $21. At current price ~$9, warrant is deeply out-of-the-money. Cash provides runway for commercialization but market remains focused on revenue delivery. |

### Outlook: **Neutral / Cautious**
The $400M raise has stabilized the balance sheet and shifted the narrative from "survival" to "execution," driving the stock's 30%+ bounce off July lows. However, the Marvell order cancellation eliminated POET's most concrete revenue catalyst, multiple class action suits remain active, and the company still has negligible operating revenue. Until POET demonstrates meaningful shipment volumes (targeting >30,000 optical engines in 2026) and converts partnerships into contracted revenue, the risk/reward remains asymmetric for all but high-risk-tolerant speculators.
