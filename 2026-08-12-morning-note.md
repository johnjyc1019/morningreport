## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures are little changed as investors brace for July's **Consumer Price Index (CPI)** — today's marquee macro event. June CPI surprised sharply to the downside (-0.4% MoM; +3.5% YoY vs. 3.8% expected), which had briefly cooled rate-hike expectations. Overnight, however, the **10-year Treasury yield climbed ~4bps to 4.705%** as oil prices surged on fresh Middle East tensions (Strait of Hormuz uncertainty). The 2-year yields 4.26% and the 30-year sits at 5.28%. Markets currently price a ~52% probability of a Fed rate hike at the September meeting, down from 67% one week ago. A hot CPI print today would sharply re-escalate those odds. Key premarket movers: **Nvidia +1.5%** (after reportedly arranging $500B in AI infrastructure bank financing) and **Intel -1.5%** (extending losses after upsizing its dilutive stock offering to $20B).

## Trade Ideas

1. **CPI vol play (bonds):** With July CPI due at 8:30 AM ET, a second consecutive downside surprise would extend the treasury rally. Short-duration bond ETFs (e.g. SHY) offer a clean expression with limited carry risk.
2. **Nvidia (NVDA) continuation:** +1.5% premarket on $500B AI infrastructure bank financing. AI capex cycle remains intact; the setup favors buying dips into the CPI catalyst.
3. **Avoid Intel (INTC) near term:** A second major dilutive raise ($20B, upsized from $15B) creates sustained overhang. Wait for technical stabilization before engaging on the long side.
4. **European energy via STOXX/FTSE:** Middle East Strait of Hormuz risk is directly lifting Brent. European oil & gas equities (Shell, BP, TotalEnergies) are benefitting; sector ETFs offer a geopolitical hedge.
5. **Cisco (CSCO) — earnings catalyst tonight:** AI networking and enterprise infrastructure demand is the key read-through. Watch guidance for data center capex signals.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price (as of Aug 10):** ~$8.56 | Recent range: $6.36 low (Jul 29) → $8.59 close (Aug 6) — a +30% bounce off the lows. No new press releases or SEC filings identified in the last 24 hours (Aug 11–12).

**Most Recent Catalyst:** Board refreshed August 1, 2026. Dr. Bardia Pezeshki (optics/photonics) and Jean F. Rankin appointed as directors; veteran director Jean-Louis Malinge resigned for personal reasons. Management is deepening its AI data center focus as it executes on its manufacturing ramp.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Current Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Marvell canceled all Celestial AI purchase orders on Apr 23, 2026, citing a confidentiality breach — POET's CFO allegedly disclosed order volumes and delivery dates in a public interview, violating NDA terms. Stock fell 46–47% on Apr 27. CFO Thomas Mika contends Marvell owes outstanding invoices and that shipments on remaining orders continue; dispute is unresolved and legally active. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | Bronstein, Gewirtz & Grossman filed a federal securities fraud class action for the class period Apr 1–27, 2026, alleging POET misrepresented its U.S. Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) tax status, exposing U.S. shareholders to adverse tax consequences they were not warned about. Lead-plaintiff deadline was Jun 29, 2026; case is now proceeding. Multiple law firms have filed similar actions. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | Completed May 2026: ~19M new shares issued at $21/share + 3-year warrants exercisable at $26.25. Stock currently trades ~59% below the $21 offering price, representing severe dilution-mark pain for May buyers. Company now has substantial cash runway for its manufacturing ramp (targeting >1M optical engine units/month by end-2027), but warrant overhang ($661M potential additional raise) remains a ceiling on any sustained rally. |

**Outlook: Neutral / Speculative.** POET's photonic integration technology is differentiated and the 2026 production ramp (>30,000 optical engines shipped this year) is on schedule. However, the stock remains well below its May capital raise price, all three key legal/financial risks are unresolved, and commercial revenue without Celestial AI must now be validated through other channels. The bounce from $6.36 to $8.56 reflects technical short-covering and board-confidence signals rather than fundamental re-rating. A definitive resolution on the Marvell dispute (either settlement or shipment confirmation) or a clean Q2 earnings beat would be the next meaningful catalysts.
