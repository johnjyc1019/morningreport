## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.81 | **Daily Change:** -5.33% | **Prev Close:** $8.25
**Market Cap:** ~$1.35B | **Volume:** ~$4.45M shares

### News (Last 24–48 Hours)
- No major press releases or SEC filings confirmed in the immediate last 24 hours. Stock continues to trade under pressure from ongoing litigation and dilution overhang.
- POET presented at CIOE 2026 (Shenzhen) in mid-September, showcasing AI-focused optical interconnects and wafer-level chip-scale packaging for Chinese data center market — small positive catalyst.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active.** Marvell acquired Celestial AI and cancelled all related POET purchase orders (~Apr 27, 2026), causing a ~46% single-day crash. Marvell is alleging POET breached NDA by publicly disclosing order details. POET's position is that disclosure was required; legal outcome uncertain — material overhang. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Rosen Law Firm and others filed federal class action suits alleging POET misrepresented its PFIC (passive foreign investment company) tax status, creating undisclosed negative US tax implications for shareholders. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026; litigation likely proceeding through discovery. |
| **$400M capital raise dilution impact** | **Significant.** POET raised $400M in a registered direct offering (19,047,620 shares + warrants at $21.00/share combined price). Warrants exercisable at $26.15 for 3 years could add another ~19M shares. Full exercise = 38M+ new shares — substantial dilution at current price levels (~$7.81 vs. $21 offering price). Stock dropped ~10–22% on deal announcement. |

### Outlook: **BEARISH / HIGH RISK**
Three concurrent headwinds — active securities litigation, NDA dispute with a major semiconductor customer, and a massive dilution overhang from warrants issued well above current price levels — create a structurally challenged setup; near-term catalysts (China AI exposure, optical interconnect demand) are insufficient to offset legal and dilution risk until there is meaningful resolution.
