**52-week range:** $3.87 – $20.81. **Analyst consensus:** Sell; $8.00 price target.

### News (last 24 hours)

- Stock continues to trade under pressure following a series of negative developments since April 2026.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Marvell (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all purchase orders on Apr 23, 2026, citing POET's alleged breach of confidentiality obligations (disclosing order/shipping details). POET disclosed Apr 27; stock fell ~47% on the news. No resolution reported. POET has not publicly disputed the NDA breach characterization. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | Multiple federal securities class action lawsuits filed in May 2026 alleging misleading statements about the company's PFIC (Passive Foreign Investment Company) tax status and confidentiality obligations. No lead plaintiff, case names, or court rulings found; litigation ongoing. |
| **$400M capital raise / dilution risk** | A $75M investment was previously secured (Oct 2025) to boost AI connectivity buildout. A separate ~$400M raise has been referenced in context of longer-term funding needs — details not confirmed in current search results; potential for significant shareholder dilution remains a concern if large-scale capital raises proceed. |

### Outlook: **Bearish / High Risk**
Despite a new Lumilens JDA ($50M initial PO, up to $500M over 5 years) providing a credible new revenue avenue, POET remains in deeply uncertain territory: the cancellation of Marvell orders directly undercuts the bull thesis that had driven the stock to $20+, class action exposure adds legal cost and distraction, and the stock's ~69% decline from its 52-week high reflects durable loss of institutional confidence. Re-rating requires clean resolution of the legal overhang and demonstrable revenue traction from Lumilens.
