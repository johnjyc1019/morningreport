## Macro Backdrop

Global risk sentiment remains fragile heading into Tuesday's open. The dominant driver is escalating **US-Iran conflict**, with reports of overnight US strikes on Iranian oil tankers following an Iranian attack on a US facility. Oil prices surged to a six-week high, with **WTI crude near $93/bbl**, pressuring equities and pushing the **10-year Treasury yield to approximately 4.80%** — a year-to-date high — while the 30-year approaches 5.30%.

Adding to the headwinds, **Canada's retaliatory tariffs** on $20B of US goods took effect Tuesday in response to US trade policy, ratcheting up trade tension alongside the Middle East conflict.

The **Fed** is in focus this week. Markets are pricing roughly a **59% probability of a 25bps hike** at the September 15–16 FOMC meeting following Friday's stronger-than-expected jobs data. **CPI for August** is due later this week; headline is expected flat at ~3.4% and core is forecast to ease slightly to ~2.4%. Any upside surprise would firmly cement rate-hike expectations.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Energy long (XLE/USO):** With WTI at $93 and US-Iran conflict showing no near-term resolution, energy names and ETFs remain tactical buys on dips. Supply-disruption premium is not fully priced.

2. **Short consumer discretionary (XLY):** LULU's warning of dramatic EPS compression signals the high-end consumer is cracking under rate and inflation pressure. Watch for read-through to other premium retail names.

3. **Defensive rotation (XLU, XLP, Gold):** A 4.8% 10-year yield + geopolitical risk + potential rate hike = classic defensive setup. Utilities underperform in a rate-hike environment but gold may see a flight-to-safety bid.

4. **SNOW call spreads:** Post-earnings momentum in AI/cloud infrastructure names can be sustained. SNOW's guide-up reflects genuine enterprise AI adoption acceleration; consider defined-risk upside exposure.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.90 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.37B
**52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81
**Daily Change:** Near flat to slightly positive (Sep 7 close); stock trading near the lower end of its range and below the 200-day SMA.

### News in the Last 24 Hours
- POET is **exhibiting at CIOE 2026** (China International Optoelectronic Expo, Shenzhen, Sep 9–11), highlighting wafer-level chip-scale packaging and AI connectivity solutions for hyperscale data centers. This is a visibility event, not a revenue catalyst, but could generate press coverage or partnership conversations.
- No new SEC filings or material press releases detected as of this morning.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **ACTIVE / UNRESOLVED.** On April 27, 2026, POET disclosed that Marvell cancelled all purchase orders tied to Celestial AI, citing confidentiality breaches stemming from an April 21 executive video interview where details about the customer relationship were disclosed publicly. Stock fell ~46% on the announcement. NDA-breach claims remain active and no settlement or resolution has been reported. |
| **(2) Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **ACTIVE / UNRESOLVED.** Multiple law firms (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, Bronstein Gewirtz) have filed or initiated class action suits covering the April 1–27, 2026 class period. Allegations include: (a) POET failed to disclose it likely qualifies as a Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC), creating adverse US tax consequences for shareholders, and (b) misleading statements about business agreements and internal controls. No court rulings or settlements reported yet. |
| **(3) $400M raise dilution impact** | **ONGOING OVERHANG.** POET announced on May 15, 2026 a registered direct offering of ~19.05M shares plus equal warrants at $21.00 per unit. With the stock now at ~$7.90 — well below the $21 offer price — the warrants are deeply out of the money but the share dilution (~19M additional shares) is structural. The raise increased the float meaningfully and remains a cap on near-term upside. |

### Brief Outlook: **BEARISH / HIGH RISK**

Three simultaneous headwinds — an active NDA lawsuit, multiple securities class actions, and structural dilution from the $400M raise — create a challenging risk-reward profile. The CIOE 2026 appearance and modest sequential revenue growth are positive signals for the underlying technology narrative, but the legal and governance overhang is likely to suppress institutional interest until there is material resolution on at least one of the open legal fronts. Risk-tolerant investors with a photonics/AI thesis may watch for a resolution catalyst, but position sizing should be minimal.
