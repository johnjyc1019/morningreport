## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures slid overnight, extending a reversal after the S&P 500 and Nasdaq posted their strongest single-day gains since April 2025. Two catalysts drove the selloff: a *Wall Street Journal* report that the administration is preparing to raise tariffs on China and dozens of additional countries, and a renewed focus on sticky inflation ahead of today's October PCE data release. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent also announced fresh Iran sanctions and Strait of Hormuz warnings overnight, pushing energy higher. The 10-year Treasury yield sits at ~4.93%, having briefly crossed 5% earlier this month after a hotter-than-expected October CPI print (headline YoY ~3.7%, core running +0.3% MoM for three consecutive months). Markets are pricing approximately 87–91% odds of a Fed rate hike in December; the Fed held rates at 3.75–4.00% at its October 28 meeting. Crude oil has been volatile — Brent was last quoted near $95–96/bbl and WTI near $89/bbl, supported by Iran risk; one source suggests a sharp intraweek pullback, so live quotes are warranted. **Watch today:** October PCE inflation, Q3 GDP second estimate, weekly jobless claims.

### Brief Outlook: **Neutral/Cautious**
POET has genuine fundamental value (AI photonics, record revenue growth, cash above market cap) but near-term catalysts remain headwinds: delivery delays pushed into 2027, Marvell overhang unresolved, securities litigation still pending, and the stock has lost ~63% from the May 2026 high near $21. A re-rating requires either a major new customer win, resolution of legal overhangs, or successful module shipments in Q4.
