## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures are little changed Friday morning after the S&P 500 slipped 0.18% to 7,709.96 on Thursday and the Nasdaq 100 fell 0.4%. The week's dominant macro narrative is the tension between decelerating inflation and a re-hawkening Fed: June CPI came in at +3.5% YoY (core +2.6%), well below expectations, but Fed Chair Warsh has signaled readiness to hike in September if incoming data re-accelerate. Markets are currently pricing ~58% odds of a September hike — down from higher levels after the Strait of Hormuz partial reopening eased oil-supply fears, though Iran has now said a formal deal will be delayed so long as US military threats persist. Brent crude jumped ~4.9% Thursday to ~$83.34, reigniting inflation concerns and pressuring bonds. The 10-year Treasury sits near 4.60%, off ~10 bps on the week. St. Louis Fed President Musalem — a hawkish voice — speaks today; Chinese trade data also releases in the Asia session.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Energy long (XLE / CVX):** Brent +4.9% on Hormuz delay; supply-risk premium likely persists near-term. Hedge exposure through Q3 with energy majors or a defined-risk call spread.
2. **Avoid chasing HUBS/APP dips:** Both guide-downs were structural (HubSpot cut full year; AppLovin missed revenue). Wait for stabilization and a reset in analyst estimates before re-entry.
3. **Short duration bias:** With Warsh hawkish, 58% Sep hike odds, and oil re-inflating, the 10-yr at 4.60% may face upward pressure. Underweight long-duration bonds; consider TBF or a short TLT position.
4. **PAYC premium capture:** Post-earnings IV spike is opportunity for premium selling (covered calls or cash-secured puts at support) if the name pulls back intraday.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.36 (Aug 4, 2026 close; +15% that session). Aug 7 intraday data unavailable at time of writing.

### News — Last 24 Hours
No major new SEC filings or press releases identified since August 4. On August 1, POET appointed **Dr. Bardia Pezeshki** and **Jean F. Rankin** to its Board of Directors as part of a governance refresh, while long-serving director **Jean-Louis Malinge** resigned for personal reasons. Company has reiterated production ramp targets: >30,000 optical engines to ship in 2026, scaling to up to 1 million units/month capacity by end-2027 across POET Infinity and Blazar platforms.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Marvell canceled all POET purchase orders April 23, 2026, citing CFO Thomas Mika's confidentiality breach (disclosure of $5M order volumes to Stocktwits). Stock fell 46–47% on April 27. NDA dispute unresolved; litigation exposure ongoing. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | Class action filed by Bronstein, Gewirtz & Grossman for class period April 1–27, 2026. Alleges POET failed to disclose probable PFIC classification, exposing U.S. investors to adverse tax treatment. Lead-plaintiff deadline (June 29) has passed; case proceeding to litigation. |
| **$400M capital raise dilution** | Completed May 2026 at ~$21/share (~19M new shares) with 3-year warrants at $26.25. Stock now at ~$7.36 — warrants deeply OTM; raise was significantly dilutive vs. current prices. Additional $150M registered direct offering also completed. Combined share issuance represents substantial dilution to pre-crisis shareholders. |

### Outlook: **Neutral/Bearish**
POET has stabilized from its post-crash lows and its AI photonics technology story remains intact, but the stock faces a credibility overhang from the confidentiality breach, active litigation on two fronts, and material dilution from capital raises executed at prices far above current levels. Absent a new marquee customer win or positive litigation development, the path of least resistance is range-bound with downside bias.
