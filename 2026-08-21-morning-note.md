## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures are under broad pressure heading into Friday's open, with bears citing elevated Treasury yields, a weakening consumer narrative (Walmart -6% on Q2 sales miss), and escalating geopolitical risk as the US-Iran ceasefire nears expiration. The 10-year Treasury yield sits at **4.70%**, near a 20-month high, as AI-driven debt issuance and widening fiscal deficits continue to pressure the long end. July CPI came in **+0.1% MoM / +3.4% YoY** — in-line with consensus — but three FOMC dissenters voted to hike at the July meeting, and Fed Chair Warsh has signaled rate hikes may not be his preferred tightening tool. Markets are now pricing a September hike as less likely following last Friday's soft jobs report.

Brent crude is trading near **$91-93/bbl** and WTI around **$85/bbl** as Iran-related supply concerns mount. US refiners have surged to all-time highs on widening crack spreads.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Short WMT / Long COST** — Walmart's Q2 sales miss vs Costco's resilient membership model suggests relative rotation within large-cap retail. Consumer is bifurcating toward value-club format.

2. **Long US refiners (PSX, VLO)** — Crack spreads at multi-year highs amid Iran supply uncertainty and constrained Russian refining capacity. Iran ceasefire expiry Monday is a near-term catalyst.

3. **Short duration / Long TIPS** — With 10Y at 4.70% and a Fed divided on the path forward, long-end yields may push higher. TIPS offer inflation protection if oil keeps running.

4. **Avoid AI hyperscalers near-term** — OpenAI growth disappointment vs Anthropic highlights competitive pressure at the top of the AI stack. Names tied to hyperscaler capex could see multiple compression.
