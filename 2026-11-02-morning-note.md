### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Marvell (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing a breach of confidentiality after POET's CFO disclosed the order publicly on Stocktwits on April 21. POET contests the cancellation's characterization. A small replacement order (~$5M) from an undisclosed third party is being cited by management, but it does not offset the lost revenue pipeline. NDA breach dispute ongoing; no settlement reported. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | Class action filed in D.N.J. (No. 26-cv-04717) on behalf of purchasers April 1 – April 27, 2026. Allegations include securities fraud tied to: (1) the Celestial AI order disclosure / stock run-up, and (2) misrepresentations about POET's Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) tax status. Stock fell ~47% on April 27 at the time of disclosure. Lead-plaintiff deadline (June 29, 2026) has passed; case is proceeding. No ruling or dismissal found as of this note. |
| **$400M capital raise dilution** | In May 2026, POET priced a registered direct offering of 19,047,620 shares + warrants at $21.00/unit (warrant exercise price $26.25). Full warrant exercise would add another ~19M shares, for ~38M total new shares. Prospectus discloses $16.08/share immediate dilution for new buyers vs. a $1.94/share pro-forma book value increase for existing holders. Stock fell 11–22% on the announcement. With the stock now trading around $5, the offering price of $21 and warrant strike of $26.25 represent major underwater positions for May subscribers. A prior $150M raise (January 2026) preceded this. |

### Outlook: **Bearish / High Risk**
POET is caught in a confluence of three unresolved overhangs — cancelled flagship order, active securities litigation, and severe dilution well above current market price — with no near-term revenue catalyst confirmed until Q3 results on November 16. The ~8% drop today in the absence of fresh news suggests continued institutional de-risking. Near-term, any positive surprise in Q3 revenue or a new customer announcement could spark a short-squeeze rally given the beaten-down price; however, the fundamental risk/reward skews negative until litigation resolves and a credible revenue replacement for Celestial AI is secured.
