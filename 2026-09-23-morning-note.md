## Macro Backdrop

Risk sentiment is cautiously constructive but geopolitics is the dominant force today. The **S&P 500** closed +0.46% on Tuesday, the **Dow** +0.56%, and the **Nasdaq 100** +0.23%, as T-note yields stabilized and New York Fed President Williams reiterated that inflation continues to trend lower. Despite surface-level calm, markets face a congested macro calendar and an escalating Middle East backdrop.

The **10-year Treasury yield** sits at ~4.96%, its highest level since early 2025, driven by oil-inflation feedback: **Brent crude is hovering near $99/bbl**, with the 30-day rolling WTI/10-year correlation climbing to 0.96 — the strongest positive relationship since June 2019. Markets now expect at least one further Fed rate hike this year; Chicago Fed President Goolsbee cautioned the central bank "cannot overlook persistent supply shocks." August PPI is forecast at +5.4% YoY; August CPI consensus is +3.3% YoY. Higher-for-longer rates continue to weigh on banks: Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan slipped in Tuesday's session.

The **UN General Assembly** is in session and a potential Trump–Xi bilateral is being closely watched for signals on AI safety, technology export controls, and tariffs. US Treasury Secretary **Scott Bessent** announced Iranian airlines are effectively shut out of international travel as of today, marking a fresh escalation in US–Iran tensions and keeping oil risk premia elevated.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Long energy majors (XOM, CVX) / Long USO** — Brent near $99 with Hormuz risk premium intact and oil/yield correlation at multi-year highs. Geopolitical escalation (Iran sanctions) provides asymmetric upside. Risk: ceasefire or diplomatic breakthrough.

2. **Short long-duration Treasuries (TLT puts / short TLT)** — Fed pricing in further hikes; oil-driven CPI adds to "higher for longer" pressure; 10-yr at 4.96% with room to move higher. Risk: recession data surprise or ceasefire.

3. **Long KB Home (KBH) / select homebuilders** — Beat on new orders signals demand resilience despite rates. Tariff-driven supply constraints are a structural tailwind for domestic builders vs. importers. Risk: further rate moves.

4. **ADBE long swing** — Upbeat revenue outlook in enterprise software signals AI monetization is real and accelerating. Constructive setup into fiscal Q4.

5. **Avoid RH and tariff-exposed consumer discretionary** — RH's explicit tariff warning signals broader pressure on imported goods retailers. Pairs well with short consumer discretionary ETF (XLY).
