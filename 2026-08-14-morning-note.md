# Morning Note — August 14, 2026

---

## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures are modestly higher ahead of the open (S&P 500 +0.09%, Nasdaq 100 +0.08%, Dow +0.05%) as markets digest an in-line July CPI print and reassess Federal Reserve rate-hike odds for September.

**July CPI:** +3.4% YoY, +0.1% MoM — second consecutive deceleration, broadly in line with expectations. Treasury yields are little changed in response.

**10-Year Treasury Yield:** ~4.68%, pulling back from a recent 19-month high of 4.75% touched earlier this week.

**Federal Reserve:** The funds rate sits at 3.50%–3.75% after three dissenters at the July meeting voted to hike. Markets currently price ~40% odds of a 25 bp hike at the September meeting, down from ~50% before this week's data. A weaker-than-expected July payrolls report last Friday reinforced the "pause" narrative.

**Energy:** Brent crude +0.13% to $87.18/bbl; WTI +0.02% to $81.27/bbl. Iran-related Strait of Hormuz tensions and Houthi disruptions of Red Sea shipping routes are keeping a modest risk premium in oil prices.

**Geopolitics:** Iran ceasefire remains fragile; Strait of Hormuz closure continues with no imminent deal in sight. Russia-Ukraine conflict ongoing with no comprehensive ceasefire agreement. US-China technology competition and trade tensions remain elevated, contributing to fragmentation risk.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$9.08 (Aug 13 close, intraday range $8.70–$9.50)
**52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81
**Market Cap:** ~$1.85B

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Resolved operationally — all purchase orders canceled April 23, 2026; Marvell cited confidentiality breach by POET executive in public interview. Stock fell 47.3% on April 27. POET is exploring legal options. |

The Q2 revenue surge (+112%) and $796M cash position provide genuine fundamental runway, but until the Marvell litigation is resolved, PFIC class actions advance, and the new Lumilens/other orders are proven with actual production shipments, POET remains a high-risk, catalyst-dependent story. The 24/7 Wall St. "147% upside" piece reflects bull-case optionality; the overhangs are real. Watch September update closely.
