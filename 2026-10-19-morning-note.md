## Macro Backdrop

September CPI came in at **3.7% YoY** (est. 3.6%), with core at **2.6%** annualized — the upside surprise keeps the Fed's December hike option firmly on the table. The Fed raised 25 bps on September 16, bringing the target range to **3.75%–4.00%**. Markets currently price ~27% probability of an October hike and ~70%+ probability of a pause, with December seen as the next live meeting. Producer prices rose 6.3% annually (+1.3% monthly in September), the largest monthly increase since March 2022, with energy the primary driver. The 10-year Treasury yield is hovering near **5.07–5.10%**, down slightly from the 5.24% peak seen October 9. Gold near $4,170 as inflation keeps tightening fears elevated. This week is earnings-heavy and macro-light, with the inflation narrative fully embedded.
