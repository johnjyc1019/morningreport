## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price (Oct 8 close):** $7.04 | **Daily Change:** -$0.38 (-5.06%) | **After-Hours:** ~$7.07
**52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.28B

### Last 24-Hour News
No new SEC filings or press releases identified since the August 13, 2026 Form 6-K (Q2 results). Q2 revenue was reported up 112% YoY. The stock continues to trade with a ~25% short float and high volatility. Next earnings: **November 12, 2026**.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Active / Unresolved. Marvell canceled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing breach of confidentiality obligations. POET stock fell ~47–51% on the disclosure. No Marvell lawsuit confirmed; POET disputes the breach characterization. New $5M PO from other clients partially offsets but does not replace lost revenue. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | Active / Pending. Multiple federal securities class action suits filed following April cancellation. Claims center on alleged misleading disclosures about: (1) confidentiality obligations and (2) potential PFIC (passive foreign investment company) tax status. Lead plaintiff, class period, and court not yet confirmed in public filings. |
| **$400M capital raise dilution** | High / Ongoing. A shelf registration of ~$400M has been flagged by analysts. Given the current ~$1.28B market cap, any material drawdown from the shelf would significantly dilute existing shareholders. Recent Seeking Alpha commentary (early October) highlights new deals arriving with "hefty share dilution." Dilution risk is live and not yet fully reflected in consensus. |

### Outlook: **Neutral/Cautious**
POET's core optical integration technology retains long-term relevance in AI data center connectivity, and Q2 revenue growth of 112% YoY shows execution is not zero. However, the Marvell/Celestial AI fallout has removed the company's most visible commercial proof point, legal uncertainty from class actions clouds the narrative, and the overhang of a $400M shelf registration caps the upside. At $7.04, the stock has partially recovered from its ~$3.87 lows but is far from the $20.81 high. Risk/reward is unattractive until: (a) legal clarity on the PFIC suits, (b) a new flagship customer replaces Celestial AI, or (c) the shelf is drawn down with proceeds deployed into visible revenue.
