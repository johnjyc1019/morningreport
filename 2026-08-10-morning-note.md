## Macro Backdrop

US equities enter Monday on a bullish footing after Friday's better-than-expected reaction to a surprise **nonfarm payrolls decline of 23,000** — the first negative monthly print in years — which pushed rate-hike odds sharply lower. The S&P 500 closed at a **record 7,758** (+0.6%), Nasdaq gained 1.3%, and the Dow added 152 points. Markets are now pricing only a ~40% probability of a Fed rate hike in September, down sharply from prior weeks.

**Fed Watch:** Chair Kevin Warsh has maintained a hawkish posture, but the soft jobs print is giving the FOMC cover to pause. June core CPI came in at 0% MoM (2.6% YoY), well below consensus — Wednesday's July CPI print is the week's pivotal data release.

**Rates & Commodities:** The 10-year Treasury yield sits at **4.67%**, elevated from earlier in the year as the Iran conflict has sustained a geopolitical risk premium. **Brent crude surged 7% to $100.69/bbl** and **WTI gained 6% to $92.19/bbl** last week as Middle East tensions reignited. Gold continues to attract safe-haven flows.

**Trade policy:** The Trump administration imposed new tariffs — 50% on certain Canadian goods, 25% on Brazilian imports, and 10–12% on ~60 additional trading partners — adding to an already fragmented global trade backdrop.
