## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures are tracking lower this morning after several major AI companies called for a slowdown in AI development, rattling a sector that has led the year's rally. The macro backdrop is further complicated by sticky inflation: August CPI came in at **+3.4% YoY headline** (core: **+2.4% YoY**, above the 0.2% consensus). The **10-year Treasury yield** sits at **4.97–4.99%**, near multi-year highs last seen in October 2023.

The **Federal Reserve** holds its policy meeting this week with a decision expected Wednesday. Markets are now pricing an **~86% probability** of a 25bps rate hike (from 72% pre-CPI), pushing the fed funds rate to **3.75–4.00%**. The August CPI print was the final major data point before the Fed vote.

**WTI crude topped $100/bbl** for the first time since May, driven by Houthi disruptions to Saudi infrastructure and a partially-closed Strait of Hormuz amid US-Iran tensions. Energy strength is feeding through to headline inflation and yield pressure.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.94 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.38B  
**Recent Move:** +112% revenue YoY in Q2 2026 (sixth consecutive quarter of sequential growth); stock has recovered from April lows but trades far below the $21 offering price from the May $400M raise.

### News (Last 24 Hours)
- No new material SEC filings or press releases identified in the last 24 hours.
- Q2 2026 results (reported earlier in September) were upbeat: revenue +112% YoY, and the company disclosed a **$50M order from Lumilens**, signaling continued optical chip demand from new customers.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell / Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | **Active / Ongoing.** Marvell canceled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026 (disclosed April 27), alleging POET breached confidentiality obligations when a company executive publicly discussed the Celestial AI relationship and shipping timelines. Stock fell 47.3% on disclosure. POET has not disclosed a settlement or resolution; litigation risk remains. |
| **(2) Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** | **Active / Consolidated.** Multiple firms (Rosen, Block & Leviton, Faruqi & Faruqi, KTMC) filed class actions on behalf of shareholders (class period: April 1–April 27, 2026). Allegations: (a) POET failed to disclose it was likely a PFIC under U.S. tax law (Wolfpack short report flagged this on April 14); (b) CFO misrepresented NDA obligations. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 — cases are likely now in the consolidation/discovery phase. |
| **(3) $400M Raise Dilution Impact** | **Structural Overhang.** Offering priced at $21.00/share (May 2026): 19,047,620 shares + warrants at $26.15. Stock is currently ~$7.94, meaning the institutional buyer is underwater by >62%. Full warrant exercise would add another 19M shares. Despite the raise funding manufacturing expansion (10x capacity target), share count dilution and the distressed institutional holder remain overhangs. |

### Outlook: **Neutral**
Fundamentals are improving (Q2 +112% revenue, new Lumilens order), but POET trades in a cloud of legal uncertainty — multiple unresolved class actions, an active NDA breach dispute with Marvell, and a large institutional holder deeply underwater. The stock needs a settlement catalyst or sustained revenue acceleration to break out. Watch for Q3 guidance or a litigation update as the next major catalyst.
