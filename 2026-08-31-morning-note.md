Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** | **Active / Post-Deadline.** Multiple class action suits (Rosen, Block & Leviton, Faruqi & Faruqi, KTMC, DJS Law Group, SueWallSt) filed in U.S. District Court (D.N.J.), class period Apr 1–27, 2026. | **3. $400M Capital Raise Dilution** | **Closed / Overhang Persists.** POET raised $400M via registered direct offering at $21.00/share (19,047,620 shares issued; 19,047,620 additional shares possible if warrants exercised at $26.15 over 3 years). At ~$7.80, shares are 63% below the offering price — warrant exercise appears unlikely near-term, limiting that overhang. |

### Outlook: **Neutral with High Speculative Risk**

POET has rebuilt ~30%+ off its July lows on genuine operational momentum (doubled Q2 revenue, new board credibility, $400M war chest for AI photonics manufacturing). However, the triple overhang of the Marvell NDA dispute, unresolved securities class actions, and massive post-raise dilution suppresses valuation re-rating. The stock requires clean legal resolution and additional customer order announcements to sustain a recovery. Analysts project ~147% upside to targets — achievable only if execution risk and legal risk clear simultaneously. **Not a conviction buy; appropriate only for risk-tolerant accounts with full awareness of litigation exposure.**
