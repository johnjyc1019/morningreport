## Macro Backdrop

The Federal Reserve held its target range at **3.75%–4.00%** on Wednesday (Oct 28) in a 10-2 vote, with Governors Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack dissenting in favor of a 25 bps hike — the most hawkish split in years. The market read the hold as a near-term relief but absorbed the dissents as a signal that further hikes remain live. The **10-year Treasury yield** sits near **5.01%** (down 2 bps from session close), having peaked at ~5.36% in early October — the highest since 2002 — on the back of a hot **September CPI print of 3.7% YoY** (vs. 3.6% expected, +0.6% MoM), driven by a 5.2% monthly jump in energy. Core prices also surprised to the upside. The macro environment is characterized by a resilient labor market, sticky inflation, and elevated rates colliding with peak Q3 earnings season.

The dominant overnight driver is **Alphabet (GOOGL)**, which missed Q3 estimates and fell nearly 10% after hours, reigniting investor anxiety about AI capex spending without commensurate revenue acceleration. That overhang is pulling Nasdaq futures sharply lower pre-open.

**Gold** is near $4,050–$4,070/oz. **Bitcoin** range-bound ~$80,000.

## Trade Ideas

1. **GOOGL puts / tech volatility hedge** — Alphabet's miss refuels the "AI revenue gap" narrative that has periodically pressured mega-cap tech. With Nasdaq futures -1.4%, a near-term volatility spike is likely. Consider defined-risk downside exposure on QQQ or GOOGL, or use VIX calls as a hedge. Do not chase the move — wait for intraday stabilization.

2. **Energy longs (XLE/CVX/XOM)** — Brent above $115, Houthi attacks on Saudi refinery, and no near-term Middle East resolution keep the energy trade intact. The Brent–WTI spread (~$13) and tightening global supply support upstream producers. Entry on any dip tied to US-Iran diplomacy headlines.

3. **Korea/SK Hynix exposure (via KOSPI ETF or HXSCL)** — The Kospi's 6.6% surge (+SK Hynix) signals that the AI memory trade is alive outside of US mega-cap names. A durable AI build-out thesis may shift attention to the memory/chip supply chain.

4. **AVOID: long duration Treasuries** — Two hawkish Fed dissents, 3.7% CPI, and ongoing supply concerns (fiscal deficit) keep the path of least resistance higher for yields. The 10yr at 5.01% does not yet price in a hiking scenario; if inflation re-accelerates, duration risk is acute.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price:** Stock was +2.98% on Oct 27, 2026 (most recent confirmed data). Q3 earnings are due **November 16, 2026**. No new filings or press releases confirmed for Oct 29 at time of writing.

**Key Risks:**

1. **Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** — *Active risk, unresolved.* In April 2026, Marvell (which acquired Celestial AI for $3.25B in Feb 2026) cancelled all purchase orders, citing POET's alleged breach of NDA confidentiality obligations (purportedly related to a public podcast interview by a POET board member/executive). The stock collapsed ~47% on April 27. No settlement or resolution found. POET secured a separate ~$5M purchase order from another customer; this does not offset the Celestial loss.

2. **Securities Class Action Lawsuits (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** — *Active, lead plaintiff deadline passed ~June 29, 2026.* Multiple federal securities class actions covering purchasers from April 1–27, 2026 allege that POET made materially false and misleading statements, including: (a) downplaying the risk that it could be classified as a Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC), which would impose onerous US tax consequences on shareholders; and (b) that the NDA disclosure led to the Marvell/Celestial order cancellation. No ruling, settlement, or dismissal found as of this writing.

3. **$400M Capital Raise Dilution Impact** — *Closed May 18, 2026.* POET raised $400,000,020 via a non-brokered registered direct offering of 19,047,620 shares at $21.00/share to a single institutional investor, with accompanying warrants at $26.25. Use of proceeds: manufacturing expansion, targeted acquisitions, R&D, working capital. Dilution is significant (~19M new shares), though the institutional backing and pricing above $21 suggests conviction at that level.

**New Positive Catalyst:** Coverage highlights a **potential $500M+ Lumilens relationship** as the key bull case — a next-generation photonics opportunity that would diversify revenue away from Marvell dependency.

**Analyst Consensus:** Average 12-month price target cited at ~$17.50 (implies ~99% upside from a sub-$9 area last close — treat this target as potentially stale).

**Outlook: NEUTRAL/SPECULATIVE.** The stock has recovered ~34.6% from its post-Marvell collapse lows, suggesting the worst-case scenarios are partially priced in. However, three unresolved overhangs (lawsuits, NDA dispute, dilution absorption) and no near-term revenue catalyst before the Nov 16 earnings make this a high-risk hold. A Lumilens deal announcement or lawsuit dismissal could be a meaningful catalyst; negative class action developments could re-test April lows.
