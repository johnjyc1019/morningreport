## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures are mixed-to-marginally-higher Monday morning after a volatile overnight session that briefly turned negative. Traders are squarely focused on **Nvidia's earnings** (after close tonight) and the anticipated **Anthropic IPO** (expected Thursday), which together represent the highest-profile test yet of AI-driven valuations in this cycle. The prior week was the best for major indices since early August — S&P 500 +2.5%, Nasdaq +2.3%, Dow +2.1% — leaving sentiment constructive but stretched.

The macro backdrop remains challenging: October core CPI printed +0.3% m/m for a third consecutive month (+0.4% headline), above consensus, keeping the Fed firmly in hawk mode. The 10-year Treasury yield sits near **5.15%**, up sharply on the week, with Fed Governor Waller floating the possibility of a **50bp December hike**. The effective Fed funds rate is 3.88%; markets are now pricing in further increases. Oil gave back ~2.5% last week, with WTI settling near $88.56 and Brent near $95.84, though geopolitical risk from US-Iran tensions keeps a floor under prices. Japan reported a surprise Q3 GDP contraction of -0.1%, and China's October retail sales and industrial output both disappointed, adding headwinds to global growth expectations.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Long Nvidia (NVDA) straddle into earnings** — Implied vol is elevated but the binary outcome (AI spend acceleration vs. guidance miss) warrants options exposure rather than a directional bet. Post-earnings direction will likely dictate near-term sector tone.

2. **Short duration / underweight long-end Treasuries** — With the 10-year at 5.15% and Fed rhetoric turning more hawkish, risk/reward for holding duration is poor. TBT (2x inverse 20+ yr) or simply avoid long-bond exposure in fixed-income allocations.

3. **Energy sector (XLE) as inflation hedge** — WTI near $88, Iran tensions unresolved, and the Fed hiking into a supply-constrained oil market supports energy names. Sector has lagged tech YTD and offers an inflation-resistant defensive tilt.

4. **Avoid Korean/Japanese chip names near-term** — SK Hynix -6% and Samsung -4% overnight suggest pre-Nvidia-earnings de-risking is concentrated in Asian semis. Tactical caution warranted until NVDA clears.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

### Price & Performance
- **Last close:** $5.08 (Nov 10, 2026), -3.52% on the day
- **52-week range:** $3.87 – $20.81
- **Q3 earnings:** Scheduled for release **today, November 16, before market open at 7:00 AM ET** — results not yet available at time of writing

### News in the Last 24 Hours
- **Q3 2026 earnings release** is the dominant catalyst today. The market will be watching for revenue trends, cash burn rate, and any update on the Lumilens/AI optics ramp.
- **New CFO Giovanni Pacelli** (formerly Silicon Labs) became effective November 2, succeeding Thomas Mika who was central to the NDA controversy.
- **Stock context:** At ~$5, shares have lost ~75% from the May 2026 offering price of $21 and ~88% from the 52-week high of $20.81, reflecting the cumulative impact of the Marvell cancellation, dilution, and litigation overhang.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Marvell cancelled all Celestial AI purchase orders on April 23, 2026 (announced April 27), citing a confidentiality breach by then-CFO Mika in a live Stocktwits interview. POET has not publicly disclosed whether it is pursuing arbitration or legal counterclaims. Order revenue impact has not been quantified. **Active / Unresolved.** |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC misrepresentation)** | Multiple firms (Rosen, Kirby McInerney, Holzer & Holzer, Gibbs Mura) filed federal class actions in D.N.J., class period April 1–27, 2026. Allegations: POET understated the likelihood of PFIC status for FY2025 and failed to disclose NDA breach risk. Lead plaintiff deadline passed June 29, 2026; case likely in early litigation phase. POET says it does not expect to be a PFIC for 2026. **Active / Early stage.** |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | May 2026 registered direct offering: 19.05M shares + 19.05M warrants at $21/share (a premium at the time). Immediate ~21% dilution; stock fell 22% on announcement. Warrants exercisable at $26.15 for 3 years. At current prices (~$5), warrant exercise is far out-of-the-money. Proceeds (~$399.7M) fund manufacturing scale-up and R&D. **Dilution already in price; warrant overhang remains.** |

### Outlook: **Neutral / Cautiously Bearish**

Today's Q3 earnings are the first real test of whether POET's Lumilens relationship and AI optics ramp are generating tangible revenue to justify the capital raise. At ~$5/share and ~75% below the offering price, much bad news is priced in — but without evidence of commercial traction, the litigation overhang and cash burn keep sentiment negative. Watch for any updated guidance on the Lumilens $50M initial PO trajectory and commentary on the legal proceedings.
