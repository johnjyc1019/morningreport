## Macro Backdrop

US midterm elections are dominating overnight price action. Early results point to a **Democratic sweep of Congress**, triggering an initial sharp selloff in futures (S&P 500 futures fell ~1%, Nasdaq 100 ~1.9%) before markets reversed and rallied sharply. As of pre-market, S&P 500 futures are indicated **+1.7%** and the Dow **+3%**.

The key macro read-through: a Democratic Congress is expected to pursue Iran war negotiations more aggressively, sending crude oil down more than **8%** to ~$96.50/bbl (WTI) — its largest single-day drop in months. This eases near-term inflation anxiety but doesn't remove it.

Inflation remains structurally elevated: the Cleveland Fed nowcast puts November CPI at **~3.92% YoY** vs ~3.74% in October. Core PCE has re-accelerated to ~3%, well above the Fed's 2% target. The Fed hiked in September 2026 — its first hike since 2023 — and the market will re-read the inflation path with oil's decline. The **10-year Treasury yield** sits at ~**4.92–5.05%**, having been volatile around the 5% level.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$5.08–5.26 (as of Nov 3–4; verify live)
**Daily % Change:** Not confirmed for Nov 5; overall trend deeply negative from 2026 highs (~$20.81 52-wk high)
**Market Cap:** ~$881M

### News in Last 24 Hours
- **New CFO appointed (Nov 2 filing):** POET named **Giovanni M. Pacelli** as CFO, effective November 2, replacing Thomas Mika (10-year tenure). Mika stays through December 2 for transition. This is POET's second major C-suite change post-crisis.
- **Q3 earnings scheduled:** POET will report Q3 2026 results on **November 16, 2026** at 7:00 a.m. ET. No other material filings detected in the last 24 hours.

### Key Risk Tracker

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | Active. Marvell canceled all Celestial AI purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing POET's disclosure of confidential order/shipping data in violation of NDA. POET stock fell 46% on the day. No resolution announced. POET has not publicly disclosed whether it is pursuing arbitration or litigation against Marvell. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | Active. At least one federal class action filed (26-cv-04717, D.N.J.) under the Securities Exchange Act, alleging misrepresentations around the NDA breach and POET's status as a Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC), which has adverse US tax consequences for shareholders. Lead-plaintiff deadline passed June 29, 2026; case is in early litigation stages. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | Severe. POET raised $400M in a registered direct offering at $21.00/share on May 15, 2026 (19M+ shares + warrants). Stock fell 22.4% on the day and has since declined further to ~$5, implying the offering investor is deeply underwater. Full warrant exercise would add another 19M shares at $26.15. Immediate per-share dilution was disclosed as $16.08 in the 424B5 prospectus. |

### Outlook: **Bearish / High-Risk Speculative**
POET enters its November 16 earnings report trading near multi-year lows, with no current revenue from its flagship Marvell relationship, active securities litigation, and a heavily diluted share count. The new CFO appointment could signal an attempt to stabilize investor relations, but structural headwinds dominate. Any positive catalyst (new customer announcements, litigation settlement, or strong Q3 guidance) could produce a sharp short-covering rally given the beaten-down price — but the fundamental backdrop remains challenged.
