### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** On April 23, 2026, Marvell (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all purchase orders with POET, citing alleged confidentiality breaches after CFO Thomas Mika publicly discussed shipment timelines tied to the Marvell relationship in a media interview. POET's stock fell 46–47% on the disclosure. Litigation risk is ongoing; no settlement or dismissal reported. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active / Post-Deadline.** Class period: April 1–April 27, 2026. Lead plaintiff deadline passed June 29, 2026. Lawsuits allege POET misrepresented its U.S. tax status (PFIC classification) and that disclosures understated the risk using equivocal language. Multiple law firms involved (Faruqi & Faruqi, SueWallSt). Cases now in early litigation stages. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | **Significant / Ongoing.** Closed May 18, 2026. POET issued 19,047,620 common shares + 19,047,620 warrants (strike: $26.25, 3-year term) at $21.00/unit to MMCAP International. Stock dropped 22% the day the deal closed. The offering more than doubled the share count, and until warrant exercise revenue materializes, dilution weight remains heavy. Proceeds earmarked for manufacturing expansion, R&D, and acquisitions. |

### Outlook: **Bearish (near-term) / Speculative**

The fundamental technology thesis (photonic integrated circuits for AI data center interconnects) remains intact, and analysts project >100% upside to consensus targets — but the execution and legal overhang are formidable. With the CFO implicated in the NDA breach that triggered the Marvell cancellation, management credibility is damaged. Three concurrent legal fronts (Marvell NDA dispute, PFIC securities class actions, general creditor risk from the capital raise covenants) create persistent headline risk. The August 10 earnings print is the next potential catalyst in either direction. **Not suitable for risk-averse investors at this stage.**
