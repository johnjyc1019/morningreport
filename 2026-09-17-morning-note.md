## Macro Backdrop

Markets remain in a high-stakes macro environment as the Federal Reserve concludes its two-day policy meeting today. Futures markets priced in a ~92% probability of a 25 bps rate hike, which would push the benchmark rate to 3.75%–4.00%. US headline CPI held at **3.4%** (August print), with core at **2.4%** and the Fed's preferred PCE gauge at **3.7%**. The 10-year Treasury yield has pushed toward the psychologically important **5%** level (briefly touching 5.04%) for the first time since 2007, pressured by elevated energy prices and persistent inflation. Crude oil remains a key driver: WTI and Brent have surged roughly **80% year-to-date**, driven by Middle East conflict threatening Strait of Hormuz supply (~20% of global oil). Separately, the Bank of Japan convenes September 17–18, with near-unanimous economist consensus expecting a **25 bps hike to 1.25%**.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$7.28 (Sep 16 close) | **Daily Change:** Not yet available for Sep 17 open
**Market Cap:** ~$1.22B | **52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81

### News (Last 24 Hours)
No new press releases or SEC filings (Form 6-K) identified specifically for September 17, 2026. Stock continues to trade in recovery mode from the April-May collapse, with the 6th consecutive quarter of revenue growth (+112% YoY) and a $50M purchase order from Lumilens cited as positives. CEO has reiterated 2026 production ramp is "on schedule."

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** On April 27, 2026, Marvell cancelled all purchase orders inherited from its Celestial AI acquisition, citing POET's breach of confidentiality obligations (management publicly disclosed PO details and shipping timelines during an April 21 interview despite being under NDA). Stock fell 46% on the announcement. Litigation risk from Marvell's NDA breach claim remains unresolved and could result in material liability. |
| **(2) Securities Class Action Lawsuits (NDA / Disclosure misrepresentation)** | **Active / Multiple Suits.** At least two law firms (Levi & Korsinsky, KTMC) have filed securities fraud class actions alleging officers made false or misleading statements about the NDA status and purchase order disclosures. Suits remain in early stages; settlement or adverse judgment would weigh on the stock and capital allocation. Note: originally characterized as PFIC tax misrepresentation suits — the primary securities fraud allegation centers on NDA/disclosure misrepresentation. |
| **(3) $400M Capital Raise Dilution Impact** | **Significant / Ongoing Drag.** In May 2026, POET raised $400M via a registered direct offering — 19,047,620 shares + equal warrants at $21.00/share. With the stock now at ~$7.28, the raise occurred at ~3x the current price, signaling severe value destruction from shareholder perspective. Warrants exercisable at $21 represent a future overhang if/when the stock recovers. Proceed-use includes manufacturing scale-up, R&D, and potential acquisitions. |

### Outlook: **NEUTRAL to BEARISH (near-term)**
Revenue trajectory (+112% YoY, Lumilens PO) and management's "on schedule" production ramp commentary provide a fundamental floor, but the legal triple-threat (NDA breach, class actions, dilutive raise at $21 vs ~$7 current) creates sustained headline risk and limits upside re-rating until courts provide clarity. Monitor for any new 6-K filings or litigation updates as primary near-term catalysts.
