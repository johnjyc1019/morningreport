## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$6.77 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.12B | **30-day Performance:** −29%

### Last 24 Hours
No new press releases or SEC filings identified for July 29. Stock continues to trade under the combined weight of the Marvell/Celestial AI fallout and ongoing class action litigation. Analysts project a 100%+ 12-month upside on the AI infrastructure thesis, but near-term catalysts are limited while legal overhang persists. Production ramp for optical engines reportedly on schedule for H2 2026, targeting up to 1 million units/month by end of 2027, and management cites 10+ active customer engagements projected to exceed $100M in annual revenue.

### Key Risk Tracker

| Risk | Status |
|------|--------|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active.** Marvell canceled all purchase orders April 23, 2026, citing a POET executive's (reportedly CFO) YouTube appearance on April 21 that allegedly disclosed confidential shipment/invoice details in breach of NDA. POET disputes the basis for cancellation. Lawsuit filed. No resolution disclosed. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active, post-deadline.** Multiple firms (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, Faruqi & Faruqi) filed class actions. Class period: April 1–27, 2026. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 — now passed. Cases are in early litigation stage. Alleged misrepresentation: POET framed PFIC classification as uncertain when it allegedly was highly probable. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | **Absorbed, but overhang persists.** POET raised $400M in a registered direct offering (May 18, 2026) at $21/share — a premium at the time. However, 19,047,620 new shares were issued plus warrants for another 19,047,620 shares (3-year expiry). Stock fell 22% on closing day. Current price (~$6.77) is now far below the $21 issuance price, meaning the single institutional buyer is deeply underwater. |

### Outlook: **BEARISH (near-term)**

Legal proceedings (class actions + NDA dispute) will suppress the stock until courts provide clarity; the loss of the Marvell/Celestial AI relationship removes a flagship revenue validation point, and the $400M raise — while providing manufacturing runway — created massive dilution at prices already far above where the stock trades today. The production ramp and 10+ customer pipeline are real but unproven. Watch for: any new customer order announcements, litigation developments, or H2 production milestone updates that could shift the narrative.
