# Morning Note — September 2, 2026

## Macro Backdrop

Global risk appetite remains challenged heading into Wednesday. The 10-year U.S. Treasury yield climbed to **4.81%** — its highest level since January 2025 — as oil prices surged on fresh Middle East escalation. Brent crude hit approximately **$101/bbl** after the U.S. struck an Iranian island in the Strait of Hormuz, reigniting supply disruption fears and rekindling inflation anxiety. Markets are now pricing in a **~60% probability** of a 25bps Fed rate hike at the September 16 FOMC meeting, up sharply from ~35% a week ago. Fed Chair Warsh and Governor Barr both reaffirmed their willingness to tighten further if inflation reaccelerates. The Strait of Hormuz disruption has temporarily removed an estimated 15–20% of global petrochemical feedstock availability since March 2026, adding structural pressure to energy and materials.

**Current Price:** ~$8.48 (last confirmed close: Aug 18, 2026)

### Last 24-Hour News
No new press releases or SEC filings confirmed in the last 24 hours. The most recent SEC filing was a Form 6-K submitted August 14, 2026. ### Key Risk Tracker

| Risk | Current Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell / Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | Ongoing and unresolved. Marvell Semiconductor (which acquired Celestial AI) issued written cancellation of all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026; disclosed April 27. Marvell cited a POET executive's April 21 YouTube interview that publicly disclosed confidential invoice and shipping timelines, violating NDA terms. Stock fell ~47% on announcement day. Civil litigation risk pending. |
| **Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** | Active. Multiple law firms — including Rosen Law Firm, Block & Leviton, Faruqi & Faruqi, Schall Law Firm, and Gibbs Mura — filed or are pursuing federal securities fraud class actions (Jones v. POET Technologies, No. 26-cv-04717, D.N.J.). Class period: April 1–27, 2026. Core allegations: (1) concealment of PFIC status, which imposes adverse U.S. tax treatment on shareholders; (2) failure to warn that executive disclosures could trigger contract termination. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 — cases are now in discovery/consolidation phase. |
| **$400M Raise Dilution Impact** | Significant overhang. POET raised $400M in a registered direct offering at $21.00/share (announced May 15, closed ~May 18, 2026), issuing ~19.05M shares plus 3-year warrants exercisable at $26.15. At ~$8.48, the stock trades ~60% below the offering price. Warrant overhang and insider lock-up dynamics add pressure. Proceeds earmarked for manufacturing expansion, R&D, acquisitions, and working capital. |

### Outlook: **BEARISH**

Multiple compounding headwinds — loss of the Marvell/Celestial AI order, active securities fraud class actions with PFIC complexity, and a deeply underwater $400M equity raise — create persistent structural selling pressure. The Lumilens order provides a potential fundamental floor if milestones are hit, but near-term recovery is difficult to underwrite given the litigation uncertainty and dilution overhang. **Key catalysts to watch:** any court filing updates in the D.N.J. class action, Lumilens engineering sample delivery confirmation, and any new customer or revenue announcements ahead of the late-2026 production ramp.
