## Macro Backdrop

Markets open Tuesday in a cautious posture as investors await the July CPI print — the week's marquee risk event — after a mixed Monday session where the S&P 500 closed little changed. The macro backdrop is dominated by three themes: (1) softening labor data reviving rate-cut hopes, (2) Middle East energy risk re-emerging, and (3) an AI earnings season that is largely rewarding large-cap leaders while punishing disappointments sharply.

- **10-Year Treasury Yield:** ~4.68%, edged down after July payrolls disappointed. Nonfarm payrolls declined 23,000 in July (vs. +80,000 expected), with May/June revised lower by a combined 103,000. Odds of a September Fed rate hike fell to ~42% from 58%.
- **Fed:** Chairman Kevin Warsh reiterated commitment to bringing inflation to a "thing of the past." Markets are pricing ~28 bps of additional tightening by December, down from 32 bps prior.
- **CPI:** June CPI came in at +3.5% YoY (vs. +3.8% expected), a positive surprise for markets. July CPI release this week is the pivotal print.
- **Energy:** WTI ~$82.38/bbl (+0.30%); Brent ~$87.69/bbl. Oil lifted ~2% on Houthi attack on Saudi Red Sea refinery and stalled Iran negotiations. Trump commented the US is "only semi-negotiating" with Iran; a Strait of Hormuz interim agreement could be announced imminently.
- **Premarket (as of writing):** SPY -0.02% at $773.10; QQQ -0.06% at $722.63. Futures modestly lower across Dow, S&P, and Nasdaq.

## Trade Ideas

1. **AMD — Hold/Add on dips.** Data Center at $6.7B and accelerating validates the AI silicon thesis outside Nvidia. Key risk: valuation premium to peers. Tactically, any CPI-driven rate backup could create a better entry.

2. **Energy longs (XLE / CVX / SLB) — Opportunistic.** Middle East risk premium is back. Houthi escalation + Iran uncertainty supports oil above $80 WTI. Energy has lagged the broader market rally YTD and offers a geopolitical hedge.

3. **Defensive rotation (XLP / XLU / healthcare) — Tactical accumulation.** Softer payrolls + elevated geopolitical risk argue for trimming cyclical beta into this week's CPI. Staples (KO, P&G) showing earnings resilience.

4. **Avoid PLTR (risk management).** The 30% day-of-earnings spike leaves PLTR at a demanding multiple. Trimming into strength is prudent; wait for a post-euphoria re-entry.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **1. Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** On April 23, 2026, Marvell (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all POET purchase orders, citing a confidentiality breach after a POET executive publicly disclosed order and shipping details in a video interview on April 21. Stock fell 47.3% on April 27 disclosure. Litigation exposure: POET risks a breach-of-NDA counterclaim from Marvell and has lost its largest near-term revenue contract. No settlement reported. |
| **2. Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** | **Active / Post-Deadline.** Multiple firms (Faruqi & Faruqi, Kirby McInerney, Rosen Law, Block & Leviton) filed or announced securities class action suits. Cases allege POET misrepresented PFIC tax status (framing near-certain PFIC classification as "may be") and failed to disclose NDA breach risk. Material legal overhang; no settlement indication. |
| **3. $400M Capital Raise Dilution** | **Closed / Overhang Persists.** On May 15, 2026, POET raised $400M via 19.05M shares + 19.05M warrants at $21/share (a slight premium to prior close). Offering closed May 18; stock fell 22%+ on close. At current ~$8.91, shares are 57% below the offering price, and the 19.05M warrants (struck at $21) are deep out-of-the-money — limiting near-term warrant exercise dilution but signaling severe market skepticism. Total diluted share count has expanded materially. |

### Outlook: **BEARISH (Speculative)**
POET has significant cash from the $400M raise to fund manufacturing scale-up, but it faces an existential customer concentration problem (Marvell/Celestial AI was its flagship design win), active securities litigation with discovery risk, and a post-raise stock that has shed most of its 2026 gains. Until a replacement tier-1 customer win is announced or legal overhang begins to clear, the risk/reward is skewed negative. Near-term catalysts are unclear; the August 3 6-K content merits review for any operational updates.
