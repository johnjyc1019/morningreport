## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Last Known Price:** ~$7.54 (Sept 14 close)  
**Market Cap:** ~$1.3B  
**52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81  
**Daily Change (Sept 14):** Not specified; confirm at open

### News (Last 24 Hours)
No new press releases or SEC filings detected as of morning note time. The most recent positive catalyst on record is a $50M initial order from Lumilens (with a potential $500M over five years) and Q2 2026 results showing revenue of $569,925 (+112% YoY) — strong growth but on a very small base.

### Key Risk Status

1. **Marvell / Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Dispute** — ACTIVE OVERHANG  
   On April 23, 2026, Marvell Semiconductor (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all POET purchase orders, citing POET's alleged breach of confidentiality obligations — specifically, publicly disclosing the existence of the Celestial AI purchase order after Marvell acquired the company. The stock fell ~46% on April 27 disclosure. No formal breach-of-contract lawsuit confirmed as filed by either party; the NDA dispute remains live and unresolved. Revenue from this customer relationship is now absent.

2. **Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)** — ACTIVE, EARLY STAGE  
   Federal securities class action alleges POET and executives made materially false statements by: (a) concealing that the company likely qualified as a Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) under U.S. tax law — a material negative for U.S. shareholders — while framing PFIC risk with equivocal language; and (b) allowing a company executive to publicly discuss confidential customer agreements. Class period: April 1 – April 27, 2026. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026. Multiple law firms filed parallel suits. Litigation is in early stages; no settlement or judgment yet.

3. **$400M Capital Raise — Dilution Already Reflected, Warrant Overhang Remains**  
   POET closed a $400M registered direct offering at $21/share (~19.05M new common shares + 19.05M warrants exercisable at $26.15 for three years). The stock fell 22% on close of the offering — dilution fears materialized immediately, and the offering price ($21) is now far above the current price (~$7.54), making warrant exercise economically irrelevant near-term. The $399.7M net proceeds are earmarked for manufacturing scale-up, R&D, M&A, and working capital — a strong strategic position if execution delivers. Note: A subsequent $150M registered direct offering also appears in filings, suggesting continued equity funding activity.

### Outlook: NEUTRAL / CAUTIOUS

The Lumilens contract and Q2 revenue growth confirm there is real demand for POET's optical engines in the AI/datacenter market. However, the Marvell revenue loss, active litigation on two fronts, and post-dilution price discovery make the stock an observation candidate rather than an active buy. A resolution on the Marvell NDA dispute or a material revenue beat in Q3 2026 would be the triggers to revisit a bullish stance. For now, the risk/reward is balanced, with downside scenario (additional litigation news, further dilution, or revenue miss) weighted more heavily than near-term upside catalysts.
