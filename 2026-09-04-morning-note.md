### Brief Outlook: **NEUTRAL / CAUTIOUS**
POET has demonstrated real revenue traction (6 consecutive growth quarters, Lumilens order) and a funded balance sheet ($400M), but the stock is trading near post-crash lows (~$7.29 vs. the $20.57 at which the raise was priced) because the legal cloud — two distinct litigation threads and the loss of its highest-profile customer — is suppressing any re-rating. A resolution of the securities class action (settlement or dismissal) or a large new customer announcement would be the next positive catalyst. Until then, the risk/reward is asymmetric in both directions: position sizing should be small.
