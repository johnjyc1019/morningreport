## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price (as of Sep 28 close): ~$7.44 | Daily change: ~+1.6%**

POET was up over 10% earlier in the week (Sep 21) as the company spotlighted AI-focused optical interconnects and wafer-level chip-scale packaging at CIOE in Shenzhen, riding the China AI data-center narrative. The stock has stabilized in the $7–$8 range after cratering from ~$20+ earlier this year.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA** | **Active.** Marvell issued written cancellation Apr 23, 2026 of all Celestial AI purchase orders, citing POET's breach of confidentiality. Stock fell 46% on the disclosure. POET is fulfilling other orders (including a ~$5M order from a separate customer). No public litigation filed between the parties as of available sources, but NDA breach claim remains an overhang. |
| **(2) Securities class actions (PFIC misrepresentation)** | **Active / Post-deadline.** Class period Apr 1–27, 2026. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026. Multiple firms (KTMC, SueWallSt, DJS Law Group) filed. Allegations: POET's 2025 Annual Report used equivocal language on PFIC classification ("may be treated"), understating the severity; and CFO Thomas Mika violated NDA by discussing business agreements publicly. Litigation is past the filing window and likely now in early discovery/lead plaintiff appointment phase. |
| **(3) $400M capital raise dilution** | **Partially absorbed.** POET raised $400M via registered direct offering at $21/share (19.05M shares + warrants at $26.15 for 3 years). A separate $150M offering was also priced. At current ~$7.44, the stock trades at a ~65% discount to the $21 issue price — the institutional investor is deeply underwater. Proceeds fund manufacturing expansion and R&D scale-up. Secondary dilution risk remains if warrants are repriced or additional raises occur. |

### Outlook: **Neutral-to-Bearish (near-term)**

POET's AI optical interconnect technology is genuinely differentiated, and the China data-center angle provides a near-term catalyst narrative. However, three concurrent legal and financial overhangs — the Marvell NDA dispute, multi-firm securities litigation, and a heavily diluted capital structure — make the risk/reward unattractive until at least one of these clears. Monitor for: SEC comment letters, court filings advancing the class actions, any settlement or licensing disclosure with Marvell, or new PO announcements that validate the commercial pipeline.
