## Macro Backdrop

The macro backdrop is defined by three forces heading into the holiday-shortened week: Middle East escalation, Fed rate-path uncertainty, and a resilient US consumer.

**Fed & Rates:** The 10-year Treasury yield settled at ~4.76%, easing from its recent three-year high of 4.81%. Fed Governor Waller signaled openness to holding rates steady if inflation continues cooling, leaving the September FOMC meeting in genuine suspense — market-implied probability of a hike sits near 50%. July core PCE printed in-line with estimates, offering modest reassurance, while July personal income and spending beat expectations. Capital goods orders disappointed.

**Inflation:** CPI data for August is expected later this week and will be the pivotal read for the September FOMC decision.

**Energy / Geopolitics:** Crude prices are firming following a weekend escalation in the Strait of Hormuz. US and Iranian forces exchanged tit-for-tat strikes on vessels, and Iran's top security official indicated Tehran plans to declare a restricted zone near Hormuz. US Secretary of Energy Wright signaled a nuclear deal may not materialize and that destroying Iran's nuclear capacity remains on the table. This keeps an energy-driven inflation tail risk squarely in view.

## Trade Ideas

1. **AVGO — Long on dips.** Broadcom's long-range AI infrastructure story (Anthropic, OpenAI) remains intact. Near-term guide softness creates a potential entry point for a 3–6 month hold into the AI capex upcycle.

2. **Energy hedges (USO / XLE).** With Hormuz tensions escalating and Iran tightening shipping lanes, a modest long energy position serves as both a geopolitical hedge and an inflation-risk hedge ahead of CPI.

3. **LULU — Avoid / watch.** The Q3 guide collapse (-61% below consensus EPS) signals a deeper demand problem. Wait for a stabilization print before considering any long.

4. **TLT (20-Year Treasury ETF) — Tactical short.** With 50/50 Fed odds, a CPI upside surprise this week could push yields back to recent highs. A small TLT short or put spread captures that asymmetry cheaply.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

### Price & Performance
- **Last close (Sep 4):** $7.77 | Market cap: ~$1.34B
- *US markets closed today; no intraday trading.*

### News (Last 24 Hours)
- No material SEC filings or press releases identified in the past 24 hours.
- POET is preparing for its exhibition at the **2026 China International Optoelectronic Expo (CIOE)**, September 9–11 — a potential positive catalyst for investor visibility and pipeline development.

### Fundamental Update
Q2 2026 revenue came in at $569,925 — up 13% sequentially and +112% year-over-year — marking six consecutive quarters of sequential growth. Net loss narrowed to -$11.3M. Production ramp for optical engines remains on schedule for H2 2026, with capacity targeting up to 1 million units/month by end-2027.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** Marvell canceled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing confidentiality breaches after a POET executive discussed order/shipping details publicly. Stock fell 46% on the news. No settlement or resolution announced. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active / Unresolved.** Multiple class actions filed (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, others) covering class period April 1–27, 2026. Allege POET misrepresented PFIC tax status and concealed NDA breach risk. Lead-plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026; cases are presumably in early litigation. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | **Structural / Ongoing.** POET raised ~$400M via ~19M shares plus warrants at $21/share (warrants exercisable at $26.15). Combined with multi-year equity issuances, shares outstanding ballooned from ~38M to ~153M in three years — a severe dilution overhang at current price levels well below the offering price. |

### Outlook: **NEUTRAL / CAUTIOUSLY BEARISH**
The fundamental optical-engine growth story (sequential revenue gains, CIOE visibility) offers a speculative long thesis, but the combination of unresolved class action litigation, a broken Marvell/Celestial AI relationship, and massive dilution at prices far above current trading levels creates a formidable headwind. Any positive catalyst near-term is likely to be absorbed by overhang selling.
