This remains the single largest overhang on the stock.

2. **Securities Class Action Lawsuits (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**  
   _Status: Active / Discovery Phase._ A federal securities fraud class action was filed April 28, 2026 (U.S. District Court, D.N.J.), covering the class period **April 1–27, 2026**. Plaintiffs allege POET and two senior executives made materially false/misleading statements regarding: (a) POET's PFIC (Passive Foreign Investment Company) tax status — a material investor concern for US shareholders of a Canadian company — and (b) the NDA/confidentiality breach that triggered Marvell's order cancellation. Lead-plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026; the case is proceeding. Material liability or settlement risk remains.

3. **$400M Capital Raise Dilution Impact**  
   _Status: Completed / Ongoing Overhang._ In May 2026, POET raised **$400M gross** via a registered direct offering at **$21.00/share** — 19.05M common shares plus 19.05M warrants (effectively doubling the warrant overhang). With the stock now trading at ~$8.48 — **60% below the raise price** — the warrants are deep out-of-the-money but represent latent dilution if the stock recovers. The cash position ($796M) provides a substantial runway but also raises questions about capital allocation strategy given the current share price dislocation vs. the raise price.

### Brief Outlook: **NEUTRAL (with negative skew)**

However, the Marvell/NDA dispute and class action lawsuits introduce binary legal risk, and the stock's 60% decline from the May raise price signals institutional skepticism. Until there is resolution on the legal front — or a new, credible anchor customer order — the risk/reward is asymmetric to the downside on negative legal developments. Monitor closely for any Q3 customer announcements or legal filings.
