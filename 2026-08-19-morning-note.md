## Macro Backdrop

Risk-off tone dominates as long-duration Treasury yields extend their surge and geopolitical tensions escalate. The **10-year Treasury yield climbed toward 4.75%** — a 20-month high — driven by surging AI-related credit issuance, sticky inflation, and energy price pressure from the U.S.-Iran standoff. July CPI came in at **+0.1% MoM / +3.4% YoY**, in-line with consensus (core also in-line), providing some relief but not enough to arrest the bond selloff. The Fed remains in wait-and-see mode: markets now price a **~52% probability of a September hike**, down from 67% a week ago following a soft July payrolls print. Oil is a key wildcard — **WTI hit $80/barrel, Brent ~$89** — as President Trump signaled no urgency to end the Iranian tanker blockade in the Persian Gulf, keeping energy inflation elevated.

Not investable at scale until litigation settles; suitable only for risk-tolerant, small-position speculation on the Lumilens ramp.
