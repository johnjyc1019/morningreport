## Macro Backdrop

Risk assets enter Monday on tentative footing as investors digest a complex backdrop: elevated energy prices, sticky inflation expectations, and renewed trade-policy uncertainty. The 10-year Treasury yield is hovering near **4.705%** — the highest since January 2025 — as oil prices have whipsawed higher on Middle East tensions. The Fed's September meeting is very much live: markets currently price roughly a **52% probability of a rate hike**, down from 67% a week ago after a softer-than-expected payrolls print. The CPI-print calendar this week remains critical, with July CPI expected to show a modest decline from 3.5% to **3.4% YoY** and core edging down to **2.5% YoY**.

Trade policy is re-entering the narrative. The Trump administration announced fresh tariffs on **Canada (50% on select goods)**, **Brazil (25%)**, and roughly **60 additional trading partners (10–12%)**. Broad corporate earnings remain resilient — 87% of S&P 500 reporters beat EPS estimates and 68% topped revenue estimates through last week — but sentiment is fragile as rising yields and geopolitical uncertainty act as an offsetting headwind.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price:** $9.26 premarket | **Change:** +3.93% from prior close of $8.91

### News in the Last 24 Hours
- **SEC Filing (Aug 14):** MMCAP International Inc. SPC and MM Asset Management filed a Schedule 13G/A reporting beneficial ownership of ~19.15 million POET common shares and warrants (9.99% of outstanding shares). Passive investment disclosure — no activist intent signaled.
- **Q2 2026 Results (Aug 13):** Revenue up 112% YoY to $569,925 — sixth consecutive quarter of sequential growth. Absolute revenue is still negligible; the growth rate is the story management is selling.
- **Board Update (Aug 3):** Dr. Bardia Pezeshki and Jean F. Rankin joined the board; long-serving director Jean-Louis Malinge resigned.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell / Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active & Unresolved.** On April 27, 2026, Celestial AI (Marvell subsidiary) canceled all purchase orders — including initial production units — citing an alleged NDA breach by a senior POET executive. The incident triggered a 47% single-day stock crash. No settlement announced; litigation ongoing. This remains the most significant near-term overhang. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Rosen Law Firm and Block & Leviton filed class actions alleging POET misrepresented its U.S. Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) status, exposing U.S. shareholders to adverse tax treatment without disclosure. Class period April 1–April 27, 2026; lead-plaintiff deadline was June 29. Cases are in early stages — no dismissal or settlement. |
| **$400M capital raise dilution impact** | **Structural Overhang.** POET closed a registered direct offering May 18, issuing 19,047,620 shares at $21.00 plus 3-year warrants at $26.25/share. At current prices (~$9), the original placement investors are significantly underwater on cost basis but hold optionality via warrants. The deal caused a 22% single-day drop on close. Share count materially expanded; any recovery from current levels faces warrant exercise pressure above $26. |

### Outlook: **Neutral / Cautiously Bearish**
The +3.93% premarket move looks like short-term momentum / short-squeeze activity rather than a fundamental catalyst. POET's operational revenue remains de minimis ($570K quarterly), legal overhangs are unresolved, and the $400M raise significantly diluted existing holders at levels well above today's price. Until there is clarity on the Celestial AI lawsuit and a credible path to commercial-scale revenue, the stock is primarily a speculative vehicle. Monitor for any surprise legal resolution or new commercial partnership announcements as potential catalysts.
