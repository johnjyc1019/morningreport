## Macro Backdrop

Markets open with a relief-rally tone after the US and Iran paused a nearly two-week exchange of military strikes. Brent crude tumbled as much as 7.4% to sub-$90/bbl, easing the inflation-via-oil channel that had been spooking rate-sensitive investors. The Fed held the funds rate at 3.50–3.75% at its last meeting (12-0 vote), dropping its prior easing bias; the CME FedWatch tool now prices a 33.7% probability of a hike at the next meeting, with a September hike at ~80%. June CPI printed cooler than expected (headline 3.5% YoY vs. 4.2% prior; core 2.6% YoY, with a flat 0% MoM reading), giving the Fed some cover to remain on hold near-term. The 10-year Treasury yield sits at 4.69–4.70%, near its highest level since January 2025, driven by tariff-related inflation concerns. Durable goods orders in June surged +1.6% (vs. -4.5% in May), signaling business investment is holding up. Earnings season continues to surprise to the upside: 86% of reporting S&P 500 companies beat EPS and 80% beat revenue, with blended YoY earnings growth of 37.9%.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Long LMT / Defense basket** — Geopolitical tension with Iran remains elevated even amid the pause; a resumption of hostilities or continued elevated defense budgets supports multi-year backlog growth. LMT's raised guidance is a green flag. RTX and NOC are also worth monitoring.

2. **Short/Avoid GEV near-term** — An EPS miss in a strong earnings season stands out negatively. Energy transition spending is uneven and GEV's miss suggests execution challenges. Watch next quarter for follow-through.

3. **Tactical oil hedge via puts on USO / short Brent** — The US-Iran pause is a relief rally, not a resolution. Any resumption would spike oil 7–10% rapidly. Cheap near-term puts could offer asymmetric protection for portfolio exposure.

4. **Caution on AI hyperscaler suppliers** — GOOGL's capex hike renewed concerns about cost discipline. Names priced on hyperscaler spending (optical, silicon photonics, custom ASICs) may see multiple compression if capex growth fails to convert to revenue. See POET section below for a sector-specific cautionary tale.

5. **REIT / Rate-sensitive watch** — If oil's pullback stabilizes inflation expectations and nudges 10-year yields modestly lower, beaten-down rate-sensitive REITs may offer a tactical bounce. Monitor the 4.65–4.70% level on the 10-year as a trigger.

### Outlook: **BEARISH — Speculative Recovery**

POET's fundamental technology (silicon photonics/EOI platform) retains potential interest from AI data center operators. The Lumilens deal is a positive proof-of-concept. However, the stock faces a trifecta of headwinds — active securities litigation, no resolution on the order cancellation, and a 38M-share potential dilution overhang from the May raise — that make near-term upside difficult to sustain. Until legal risks are quantified, a new CEO is named and shows customer traction, and the dilution overhang is absorbed, risk/reward favors staying on the sidelines or maintaining a small speculative position only.
