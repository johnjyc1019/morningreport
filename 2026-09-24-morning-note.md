## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** $7.99 (as of September 23 close) | **52-Week Range:** $3.87 – $20.81

**Daily Change:** Slight decline from prior session; trading near multi-month lows well off the $20.81 52-week high.

**Recent News (Last 24 Hours):** No material new press releases or SEC filings detected for September 24. The stock continues to trade in a depressed range as investors digest the compounding negative catalysts from April–June 2026.

---

### Key Risk Status

**(1) Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit**
Marvell Semiconductor (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing POET's alleged breach of confidentiality obligations. POET executives had disclosed shipping timelines and invoice details for the Celestial AI relationship in a public YouTube interview on April 21, 2026. The disclosure triggered the cancellation and the stock fell 47.3% on April 27. No resolution disclosed; legal exposure remains open. **Status: Ongoing — unresolved, material overhang.**

**(2) Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**
Multiple class action lawsuits were filed beginning April 28, 2026 (U.S. District Court, District of New Jersey) covering the class period April 1–27, 2026. Allegations include: (a) POET misrepresented its PFIC (Passive Foreign Investment Company) tax status to US investors—the company disclosed in its 2025 Annual Report it "may be treated" as a PFIC, which plaintiffs argue understated confirmed PFIC classification; and (b) the NDA breach that led to order cancellation. Firms involved: Rosen Law Firm, Block & Leviton, and others. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026. **Status: Active litigation — no settlement or dismissal disclosed.**

**(3) $400M Capital Raise Dilution Impact**
In June 2026, POET raised $400M via a registered direct offering of 19,047,620 common shares + warrants at $21.00/share (premium to then-market). Full warrant exercise (at $26.15, 3-year term) would add another 19,047,620 shares, totaling up to ~38M new shares. The offering caused the stock to drop 22.36% on announcement. Proceeds are earmarked for manufacturing expansion (~10x capacity), R&D, and acquisitions. Given current price of ~$8, this raise was completed near the 52-week high and represents significant dilution at the current depressed valuation. **Status: Capital raised and closed; ongoing dilution risk from warrant overhang.**

---

### Brief Outlook: **BEARISH**

POET remains in a deep technical and fundamental hole. The Marvell order cancellation removed the company's most visible commercial validation just as securities fraud litigation piles on. The $400M raise—executed at $21.00, now trading at $8—means institutional investors are deeply underwater and warrant exercise is highly unlikely at current prices, limiting near-term capital conversion. Until POET secures a credible replacement customer win with verifiable production orders and achieves litigation clarity, the risk/reward skews negative.
