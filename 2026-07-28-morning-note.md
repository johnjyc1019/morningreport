## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$6.99 (as of July 24, 2026) — approximately −32% from the June 30 high of $10.28; down ~47–50% from the pre-crisis levels in April.

**Recent News (last 24h):** No confirmed new filings or press releases dated July 28. The most recent major headline (July 24) noted continued class action escalation and stock weakness.

### Key Risk Status

1. **Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit:** *Active / Unresolved.* On April 23, 2026, Marvell (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all POET purchase orders, citing confidentiality breaches — an executive discussed shipping and invoice details in a YouTube interview shortly after a short-seller report. The disclosure on April 27 caused a −47.3% single-day collapse. Litigation ongoing; no settlement or dismissal reported.

2. **Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation):** *Active / Escalating.* Multiple law firms (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, Faruqi & Faruqi, Kirby McInerney, DJS Law Group) filed class actions alleging POET misrepresented its Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) tax status in its 2025 Annual Report, using equivocal language to downplay what plaintiffs argue was near-certain classification — imposing punitive US tax treatment on American shareholders. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026; consolidation phase likely underway.

3. **$400M Capital Raise Dilution:** *Closed / Ongoing Overhang.* On May 18, 2026, POET closed a registered direct offering at $21.00/share (at a premium to market), issuing 19,047,620 new shares plus warrants for 19,047,620 additional shares at $26.25 (3-year exercise window). The deal raised gross proceeds of $400M for manufacturing expansion, R&D scaling, and targeted acquisitions. Stock fell 22% on closing day. The full warrant tranche represents another ~25–30% potential dilution above the current float.

### Outlook: **BEARISH (Near-Term)**

The stock is caught between genuine long-term optionality (revenue +202% YoY in Q1 2026; optical engine ramp toward 1M units/month by end-2027) and a compounding legal/governance overhang. Without a lead plaintiff settlement or a new anchor customer announcement, near-term catalysts are limited while litigation uncertainty caps institutional re-entry. The warrant overhang at $26.25 is far above current levels and unlikely to be a near-term technical factor, but any recovery rally toward $10+ will face activist-short pressure and dilution fears.
