# Morning Note — August 28, 2026

## Macro Backdrop

Risk assets closed Wednesday's US session firmly in the green — S&P 500 +1.45%, Nasdaq +1.74%, DJIA +0.85% — powered by Nvidia's blowout quarter and a broad semi/tech lift. Overnight the picture is mixed: Asia edged higher on the coattails of the US rally while European bourses are giving back some gains as oil prices and inflation data reassert themselves.

The macro overhang remains elevated. The July PCE deflator printed 0.2% MoM vs. 0.1% est., pushing the year-over-year reading to 3.7% (est. 3.6%). Fed funds futures now price a ~52% probability of a September hike (down from 67% a week ago after prior session Treasury buying), but the direction of travel is still hawkish. The 10-year Treasury yield holds at **4.64–4.66%**. Oil is not helping: WTI at **$83.20/bbl** and Brent at **$88.91/bbl** continue to stoke cost-push inflation fears as Middle East tensions keep the Strait of Hormuz risk premium elevated.

Geopolitically: US-China tech/trade friction continues to be the dominant theme for capital allocation in August, with investors rotating into defensive and geopolitically-advantaged sectors.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Long NVDA / Semi ETFs (SOXX)** — Nvidia's beat confirms accelerating data center capex. Pullbacks toward $950–980 offer entry; momentum favors the semi complex near-term.
2. **Long VEEV** — Consistent beat-and-raise in a defensive SaaS vertical (life sciences). A 16% gap-up with raised guidance suggests fundamental re-rating, not just short-covering.
3. **Avoid INTU near-term** — Guidance miss in a rate-elevated environment is doubly punishing; wait for a reset to cheaper multiples before re-entering.
4. **Fade European weakness selectively** — DAX -0.62% overnight looks like profit-taking rather than structural deterioration; Germany-exposed industrials with clean balance sheets may offer a tactical bounce.
5. **Oil-linked energy names cautious** — WTI at $83 supports E&P cash flows, but the same price level stokes Fed hawkishness. Net neutral; prefer quality FCF names with dividend coverage.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$8.09 (Aug 26 close); 52-week range: $6.36 (Jul 29 low) – $8.59 (Aug 6)  
**Daily Change:** Approximately flat to slightly negative in recent sessions after a >30% bounce off July lows.

### News (Last 24 Hours)
No major new filings or press releases identified in the last 24 hours. The August 1 board additions of Dr. Bardia Pezeshki (optics expert) and Jean F. Rankin (industry veteran) remain the most recent material announcement. Management commentary around H2 2026 production ramp (targeting ~1M optical engine units/month by end of 2027) and $830M in cumulative equity raised over 12 months has been the narrative backdrop supporting the bounce from lows.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** On Apr 27, 2026, Marvell (acquirer of Celestial AI) cancelled all POET purchase orders, citing confidentiality breaches. POET stock fell 47% that day. Litigation and/or NDA dispute ongoing; no settlement announced. |
| **(2) Securities class action (PFIC / tax misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Complaint filed Apr 28, 2026 in USDC NJ alleging material misstatements during Apr 1–27, 2026 class period. Discovery phase likely; outcome remains highly uncertain. |
| **(3) $400M raise / dilution impact** | **Significant dilution in place.** Company raised ~$375M+ in equity ($225M+ in Q4 2025, $150M in Jan 2026). Share count has expanded materially. Any further capital needs would add further dilution pressure. |

### Outlook: **Neutral / Cautiously Bearish**
The bounce off $6.36 reflects some relief that the company is still executing operationally and building board credibility in photonics, but the three active legal/financial overhangs (order cancellation, class action, and dilution from repeated capital raises) limit the risk/reward at current levels. Without a new anchor customer announcement or litigation resolution, the stock is likely to remain range-bound or drift lower.
