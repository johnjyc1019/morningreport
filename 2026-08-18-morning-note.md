### Key Risk Tracker

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell / Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** Marvell cancelled all Celestial AI purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing confidentiality breaches by a POET executive (YouTube interview disclosing invoice/shipping details). Stock fell 47.3% on disclosure. Civil litigation exposure remains; no settlement disclosed. |

| **(3) $400M raise dilution impact** | **Significant / Ongoing.** May 18, 2026 registered direct offering issued 19,047,620 shares + 19,047,620 warrants (exercise price $26.25, 3-year term) to MMCAP at $21.00/share. Stock is currently at $5.09 — 76% below the offering price. Warrants deeply out-of-the-money. Existing shareholders absorbed severe dilution; warrants add future overhang if stock recovers toward $26.25. |

### Outlook: **Neutral / Speculative — High Risk**

POET's Q2 revenue growth (112% YoY) and the Lumilens order provide genuine fundamental support, but the stock is caught between three compounding overhangs: active securities litigation, severe post-raise dilution (shares at $5.09 vs $21 raise price), and unresolved reputational damage from the Celestial AI cancellation. Until litigation risk is quantified and the company demonstrates the Lumilens order converts to sustained revenue, risk/reward remains skewed to the downside for most investors. Speculative buyers should size positions accordingly.
