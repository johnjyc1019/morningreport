## Macro Backdrop

Risk appetite improved overnight after Tuesday's session saw the S&P 500 print a fresh record close above 7,800. Markets are balancing a resilient earnings backdrop (62% of S&P 500 companies guiding positive EPS for Q3, well above the 5-year average) against persistently elevated macro headwinds: the 10-year Treasury yield sits at **5.28%** — a level last seen in 2002 — and the Fed delivered its first rate hike since 2023 on September 16, pushing the funds target to **3.75%–4.00%**. Oil is sticky; front-month WTI and the 10-year yield are running a 0.96 correlation, the tightest since June 2019. September CPI (headline 3.4% / core 2.4% YoY as of last print) keeps the Fed on guard. Ongoing US–Iran tensions and tightening semiconductor export controls to China add to tail-risk pricing.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** ~$8.00 | **Daily Range (Oct 6):** $7.84–$8.19 | **52-Wk Range:** $3.87–$20.81

### Last 24 Hours
No fresh press releases, SEC filings, or analyst notes observed since October 6. The stock remains in a prolonged consolidation following a catastrophic spring/summer. Wall Street Zen downgraded to **Sell** on September 26.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active.** April 27, 2026: Marvell cancelled all purchase orders, alleging POET breached confidentiality obligations when a senior executive publicly disclosed order details. Stock fell 47.3% on the news. NDA dispute is unresolved and represents material legal/reputational liability. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Multiple suits filed in the District of New Jersey (lead complaint April 28, 2026) allege POET made materially false statements about its PFIC tax status and that an executive violated an NDA in a public interview. Class period: April 1–27, 2026. Litigation overhang suppresses institutional interest. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | **Ongoing.** May 2026 registered direct offering at $21/unit (19.05M shares + warrants exercisable at $26.25 through May 2029). Stock fell 22% on announcement; current price at $8.00 is 62% below the offering price, making warrant exercise effectively dead. Dilution risk is real but the ~$796M cash position extends runway. |

### Outlook: **BEARISH / NEUTRAL**
POET's cash runway is substantial and the Q2 net loss did narrow ($11.3M vs. $12.3M), but the company has lost its flagship Marvell/Celestial AI customer, faces unresolved securities litigation, and its stock is trading near its 52-week trough. The recovery to $8 from a low of $3.87 reflects some stabilization, but without a credible replacement anchor customer and resolution of the lawsuits, the risk/reward remains unfavorable. The $50M initial order under the Lumilens framework is a positive signal but not yet a catalyst large enough to offset the structural headwinds.
