## Macro Backdrop

US equities are limping into Friday's open after a brutal two-session selloff. The Dow closed below 50,000 for the first time since June on Thursday, dropping 2.19% to 49,097. The S&P 500 fell 2.02% to 7,175 and the Nasdaq shed 2.25% to 23,879. The proximate catalyst was Anthropic's public market debut, which failed to revive the AI trade — shares closed below their IPO price, triggering broad selling in AI-linked names. The Dow is on pace for its worst weekly performance since April 2025, down ~6.2% week-to-date.

The macro backdrop remains hostile. October CPI printed hot for a third straight month: headline +0.4% MoM (est. 0.3%) and core +0.3% (est. 0.2%), with super-core services rising at a 5.5% annualized pace. PPI surged 6.5% YoY, the largest annual gain since November 2022. The Fed (rate: 3.75–4.00%) meets December 9, and markets are pricing a ~90% probability of a 25 bps hike. The 10-year Treasury yield sits at ~5.10%, near multi-year highs, while the 30-year hovers around 5.45%. Brent edged up 0.3% to ~$92.20 on Houthi attacks targeting Riyadh, but crude remains on track for a third straight weekly decline.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Fade the AI bounce (short NVDA/AMD into strength):** The Anthropic IPO disappointment signals that AI valuations may have gotten ahead of near-term demand. Any morning bounce in semiconductor/AI names driven by oversold conditions is a sell. Risk: a genuine policy catalyst (e.g., softer Fed tone) could squeeze shorts hard.

2. **Energy over-correction watch (long XOM/COP on dips):** Brent is +0.3% on Houthi strikes but still heading for a weekly loss. If Middle East tensions escalate further, energy majors with strong free cash flow offer a hedge vs. the broader equity weakness. Risk: OPEC+ could signal supply increase at the Nov 29 meeting.

3. **Short duration bias (TBT or underweight long bonds):** With October CPI coming in hot for a third straight month and December hike nearly fully priced, the path of least resistance for yields remains higher. Avoid extending duration until the December 9 Fed decision.

4. **Defensive rotation (XLV, XLP):** With the Dow down 6.2% on the week, oversold conditions may favor defensives — healthcare and consumer staples — relative to tech into year-end.
