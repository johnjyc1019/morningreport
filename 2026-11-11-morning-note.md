# Morning Note — 2026-11-11

## Macro Backdrop

October CPI came in hotter than expected (+0.4% MoM headline vs. +0.3% est.; core +0.3% MoM vs. +0.2% est.), rattling markets on November 10. Fed Governor Waller signaled a "stronger policy move may be needed," pushing markets to price ~90% odds of a 25bps December hike and ~10% odds of 50bps. The 10-year Treasury yield surged from 4.88% to nearly 5.00%, briefly crossing that threshold for the first time since early October. Gold fell more than 2% on the rate-hike repricing. Brent crude fell 1.2% to ~$91.49 on hopes of US-Iran peace talks, but Iran's claim that tankers were struck by mines in the Strait of Hormuz remains a live escalation risk. **Note:** Bond markets are closed today (Veterans Day); US equity markets are open.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Short Treasuries / Long Vol** — Hot CPI raises probability of a larger December hike. Consider TBT (2x short Treasuries) or put spreads on TLT if inflation remains sticky. Risk: data reversal or geopolitical risk-off.

2. **Long CoreWeave (CRWV)** — Strong beat and guidance raise in hyperscale AI infra; direct beneficiary of ongoing AI capex cycle. Risk: expensive valuation, Nvidia supply chain concentration.

3. **Cautious on Nvidia (NVDA)** — After -6% on Nov 10 amid AI regulation concerns and macro headwinds, risk/reward is less favorable near-term. Watch for consolidation before re-entry.

4. **Energy hedge via Brent** — Iran-conflict premium could re-widen quickly if the Strait of Hormuz escalation materializes. Consider UCO or XLE as a tactical portfolio hedge.
