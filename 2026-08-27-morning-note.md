## Macro Backdrop

US equities closed nearly flat Tuesday ahead of Nvidia's report: S&P 500 –0.02% (7,676), Dow –0.21% (53,464), Nasdaq –0.08% (26,130). The VIX eased to 15.21, suggesting contained fear despite an active geopolitical backdrop.

**Rates:** The 10-year Treasury yield settled at ~4.65%, pulling back from a recent 20-month high of 4.75% as oil prices declined and near-term inflation fears softened.

**Oil:** Brent crude fell 2.8% to $86.08/bbl and WTI dropped 3.0% to $79.93/bbl after the US signaled a preference for tightened Iran sanctions over expanded military action.

**Fed:** Markets are pricing a ~52% chance of a September rate hike, down from 67% a week ago, following softer labor data. The Jackson Hole symposium continues to be in focus for clues on the Fed's forward path.

**Key Risk:** Middle East tensions remain elevated. President Trump has threatened Oman with military action and signaled no urgency to end the Iran conflict. Fresh Lebanon violence is adding a geopolitical risk premium across energy and defense names.

**Current Price:** $8.48 (last reported as of Aug 18, 2026) | **Market Cap:** ~$1.5B

_Confirm live price at open — stock has been volatile since April events._

### News in the Last 24 Hours
- No material new press releases or SEC filings identified overnight (most recent: Form 6-K filed Aug 14, 2026 re: Q2 2026 results and registration statement filed Aug 13).
- Q2 2026 results (reported Aug 13): Revenue **+112% YoY** — sixth consecutive quarter of sequential growth. Lumilens $50M order highlighted as key demand signal.
- Analyst consensus price target: ~$21.14 (implied ~150% upside from current levels).

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell / Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** Marvell canceled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing confidentiality breaches after a POET executive publicly discussed order/shipping details. Stock dropped ~47% on April 27. NDA breach litigation is ongoing. No settlement reported. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Multiple firms (Faruqi, Rosen, Kirby McInerney, Block & Leviton) filed class actions alleging POET misrepresented its PFIC status under US tax law, creating hidden liabilities for US investors. Class period: Apr 1–27, 2026. Lead plaintiff deadline passed (Jun 29). Cases now in early litigation phase. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | **Closed / Ongoing overhang.** Raised $400M via registered direct offering (May 18, 2026): 19.05M shares + 19.05M warrants at $21.00 combined ($26.15 warrant strike). Total potential dilution ~38M shares. Proceeds earmarked for manufacturing scale-up, M&A, and R&D. Stock has since retraced significantly below the offering price — warrants are deeply out of the money. |

### Outlook: **NEUTRAL with downside risk bias**

Revenue growth is genuine (6 consecutive beats, Lumilens order), but the Marvell NDA fallout has severed the company's most visible near-term revenue pathway, securities litigation adds headline and potential liability risk, and the $400M raise was transacted far above the current price, signaling that equity holders who participated are underwater. Watch MRVL's earnings call today for any indirect commentary on POET or AI optical supply chains.
