## 1. Macro Backdrop

The macro environment heading into September month-end is a study in stubborn resilience. The Fed raised its benchmark rate 25 bps to **3.75–4.00%** at its September meeting, with Chair Kevin Warsh noting that higher Treasury yields are being driven by economic strength, capital competition, and geopolitical factors rather than purely by monetary policy. CPI remains well above the Fed's 2% target, and markets are now pricing in nearly **100 bps of additional hikes** over the next 12 months — a hawkish repricing that has pushed the **10-year Treasury yield to 5.25–5.28%**, levels not seen since mid-2007.

Geopolitical risk is the secondary overhang. The US–Iran standoff shows no signs of de-escalation — President Trump has reportedly rejected a Strait of Hormuz peace deal from Tehran, keeping crude prices elevated. Qatari mediators are in active talks, but markets remain skittish. The combination of higher-for-longer rates and a geopolitical premium in energy is creating a challenging backdrop for rate-sensitive equities.

## 6. POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current Price:** $7.71 | **Daily Range:** $7.49–$7.93 | **Market Cap:** ~$1.33B

### Last 24 Hours
No major new filings or press releases detected as of morning. Stock appears to be consolidating following a period of intense corporate activity over the prior five months. Q2 2026 revenue came in at $569,925 (+13% QoQ, +112% YoY) — the sixth consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth, though absolute revenue remains minimal relative to the company's market cap. The $796M cash balance (post $400M raise) provides a long runway.

### Key Risk Status

**(1) Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit**
Marvell canceled all Celestial AI-linked POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing breach of confidentiality obligations after a POET executive publicly disclosed deal details in an interview. POET stock fell 47.3% on April 27. The NDA lawsuit risk remains open — no settlement or resolution reported. **Status: Active / Unresolved.**

**(2) Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**
Multiple class actions were filed against POET and two executives covering the class period April 1–27, 2026. Allegations: (i) POET understated the likelihood of PFIC classification in SEC filings, exposing US shareholders to adverse tax treatment; (ii) the CFO violated a customer NDA. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026. Litigation consolidation is likely ongoing. **Status: Active / Pre-discovery.**

**(3) $400M Capital Raise — Dilution Impact**
POET closed a $400M registered direct offering in June 2026 at $21.00/share, issuing 19,047,620 new shares plus warrants exercisable at $26.15 for three years. The stock fell 22% on closing-day dilution shock. Current price of $7.71 is well below both the offering price and the warrant strike — implying the offering investor is deeply underwater and warrants are far out-of-the-money. Warrant overhang at $26.15 is not a near-term risk at current levels, but capital structure complexity remains a headwind. **Status: Dilution baked in; warrant overhang dormant at current price.**

### Recent Positive Catalysts
- $50M Lumilens opening order + $500M five-year framework agreement signal early commercialization traction.
- Six consecutive quarters of YoY revenue growth.
- $796M cash war chest funds manufacturing expansion and R&D acceleration.

### Outlook: **Neutral (Speculative)**
POET is a binary execution story. The cash cushion is real, the Lumilens order validates the technology, and revenue is growing — but from a near-zero base. The Marvell NDA lawsuit and PFIC class actions introduce binary legal risk, management credibility is impaired after the April events, and the stock at $7.71 is 63% below the $21.00 offering price — a stark disconnect between management's capital-raise confidence and current market sentiment. Hold for now; wait for Marvell lawsuit resolution or Lumilens order ramp proof before adding.
