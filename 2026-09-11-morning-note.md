# Morning Note — September 11, 2026

## Macro Backdrop

US equity futures open modestly mixed on the 25th anniversary of September 11. S&P 500 futures edge up 0.12%, Dow futures gain 0.20%, while Nasdaq futures slip 0.12% — a tentative bounce after Wednesday's session in which the S&P 500 fell 0.6%, the Nasdaq 100 lost 1.3%, and the Dow shed 0.4%.

The overriding macro theme is the US-Iran military conflict, which has driven WTI crude above $100/bbl for the first time since 2023. US airstrikes on IRGC targets and Iranian missile exchanges in the Persian Gulf have rattled the Strait of Hormuz, temporarily removing an estimated 15–20% of global petrochemical feedstock supply and lifting energy stocks while pressuring broader equities.

The Federal Reserve's September meeting is the other live variable. July CPI printed at +3.4% YoY (in-line), leaving traders pricing roughly 52% odds of a rate hike — down from 67% the prior week. August CPI is due imminently and will be the final major input before the decision. The 10-year Treasury yield stands at 4.705% and the 30-year at 5.251%.

## Trade Ideas

1. **Long Energy (XLE / individual E&P):** WTI above $100 with Strait of Hormuz disruptions and no near-term diplomatic resolution makes energy the standout sector. Integrated majors benefit from both upstream margin and refining spreads; XLE provides diversified exposure.

2. **Short LULU on the bounce:** A 20% gap down on a genuine earnings miss and severe guidance cut is not a reflexive fade opportunity — it is a re-rating event. Consumer discretionary faces a double squeeze from elevated rates and weakening discretionary spend. Any dead-cat bounce into $50–55 resistance may offer a short entry.

3. **Defensive rotation — Utilities / Gold:** If the Fed hikes in September, rate-sensitive growth equities face another de-rating. Gold has historically performed well in military conflicts; utilities offer yield-alternative characteristics if the hiking cycle is near its end.

4. **Avoid / Reduce POET (POET):** See the dedicated section below. Compounding legal and dilution overhangs make the risk/reward unattractive until a credible new order book is established.

## Key Events to Watch

- **August CPI** (due today or imminently): The critical final input for the September FOMC decision. July's 3.4% YoY print was in-line; a hotter-than-expected read would push rate-hike odds back above 60% and likely sell off Treasuries and rate-sensitive equities.
- **September FOMC Meeting** (~Sept 16–17): Rate decision with ~52% market probability of a hike. Any hawkish surprise after a soft CPI would be a significant market shock.
- **US-Iran Conflict:** Any formal closure or mining of the Strait of Hormuz, or escalation to direct naval engagement, would spike oil and trigger broad risk-off. Monitor CENTCOM statements.
- **Xi-Trump Summit** (scheduled this month): Trade and Taiwan framework; significant announcements would re-price geopolitical risk premium across EM and tech supply chains.
- **Earnings pre-announcements:** With elevated oil costs and tariff headwinds, watch for Q3 profit warnings from logistics, airlines, and consumer-facing companies.

The Lumilens $50M order is a positive but insufficient to offset the revenue hole left by the Marvell/Celestial AI relationship. **Re-rating catalysts to watch:** new Tier-1 customer order announcements, class action dismissal or favorable early rulings, or a meaningful step-up in Lumilens and CIOE pipeline conversion. Absent those, downside risk persists.
