## Macro Backdrop

Risk-on tone dominates Monday's open as US equity futures surge overnight, with S&P 500 E-mini futures up ~0.95% to 7,730 and Nasdaq futures also higher, led by NVIDIA, Amazon, and Alphabet. The macro backdrop remains complex: the Fed hiked rates for the first time in three years at its September 16 meeting, bringing the overnight lending rate to 3.75%–4.00%, with Chair Kevin Warsh signaling persistent inflation risks and flagging two total hikes in 2026 (next expected in December). The 10-year Treasury yield sits near **5.04%**, its highest since 2007, driven by elevated oil prices and fiscal concerns. Crude prices remain elevated amid Middle East tensions — both the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab remain contested, with Saudi Arabia conducting over 300 strikes on Yemen in recent weeks. An OPEC+ ministerial meeting is scheduled for October 4. The macro calendar for Monday is relatively light.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Price:** ~$7.80 | **Daily % Change:** +4.8% from intraday low (52-wk range: $3.87–$20.81)

### News (Last 24 Hours)
- POET shares recovering modestly; Q2 results were upbeat with new optical chip orders reported.
- New partnership with Lumilens noted by analysts as a positive catalyst.
- No major new SEC filings identified as of this writing.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **(1) Marvell/Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active.** Marvell cancelled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing confidentiality breaches (executive disclosed NDA-protected customer details). Stock collapsed 47% on April 27 disclosure. Lawsuit pending in U.S. District Court (NJ); outcome uncertain. Major revenue disruption. |
| **(2) Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active.** Multiple class actions filed (Rosen Law, Block & Leviton, others) over alleged failure to disclose PFIC status for U.S. tax purposes. Wolfpack Research short report (April 14, 2026) triggered the chain of events. POET subsequently confirmed PFIC status and announced plans to redomicile to the U.S. Litigation exposure remains high. |
| **(3) $400M capital raise dilution** | **Significant dilution.** POET raised $400M via registered direct offering at $21.00 per share+warrant (19.05M shares + 19.05M warrants). Offering executed at a significant premium to current price ($7.80), indicating severe stock degradation since raise. Full warrant exercise would near-double the share count — substantial ongoing dilution risk. |

### Outlook: **NEUTRAL/BEARISH**
Despite encouraging Q2 results and a new Lumilens partnership, POET faces an existential-level legal and operational reset: its largest disclosed customer relationship is severed, multiple class actions are active, and the dilutive raise has compressed the share price ~63% below the offering price. Recovery requires new customer wins and legal resolution — neither is imminent.
