Risk assets are navigating a regime of higher-for-longer rates, geopolitical uncertainty, and mixed corporate earnings signals.

## POET Technologies (POET) Daily Update

**Current price (as of ~Sept 24-30, 2026):** ~$7.75–$7.80 | **52-week context:** Near lows; below 200-day SMA

### Last 24 Hours
No major new filings or press releases detected in the last 24 hours. POET's most recent 6-K filings cover Q2 2026 results (revenue +112% YoY, sixth consecutive quarter of sequential growth) and the $400M financing close. The stock has stabilized at depressed levels after the April-June collapse but remains under the weight of multiple unresolved legal and execution risks.

### Key Risk Status

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| **Marvell / Celestial AI order cancellation & NDA lawsuit** | **Active / Unresolved.** Marvell canceled all POET purchase orders on April 23, 2026, citing POET's breach of confidentiality obligations after CEO disclosed Celestial AI product details in a YouTube interview on April 21. POET stock fell **47.3%** on April 27. Underlying business loss is material; NDA litigation risk persists. |
| **Securities class actions (PFIC tax misrepresentation)** | **Active / Lead plaintiff phase likely concluded.** Multiple law firms (Rosen Law Firm, Block & Leviton) filed class actions covering the April 1–27, 2026 period, alleging POET misrepresented its PFIC tax status and that the NDA breach was a foreseeable disclosure risk. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026 — cases are now proceeding toward discovery/motion phase. Liability exposure unquantified. |
| **$400M raise dilution impact** | **Overhang remains.** POET closed a $400M registered direct offering at $21.00/share (19,047,620 shares + warrants for another 19,047,620 shares at $26.15, 3-year term). With stock now at ~$7.80 — well below both the offer price and warrant strike — the raise funded manufacturing expansion (~10x capacity) but the dilution overhang and loss of the CFO (a decade-long tenure) weigh on sentiment. |

### Outlook: **BEARISH (Near-Term)**

Three compounding headwinds — loss of Marvell/Celestial AI revenue, active securities litigation, and ~38M diluted share overhang with stock trading 63% below the offering price — create significant near-term pressure. The bull case (Lumilens $50M opening order + $500M 5-year framework; $796M cash position) requires execution on new customer design wins while managing legal distraction. Until at least one lawsuit resolves or a major new customer commitment is disclosed, risk/reward skews negative.
