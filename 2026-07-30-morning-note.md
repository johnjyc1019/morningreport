### Key Risk Status

**(1) Marvell/Celestial AI Order Cancellation & NDA Lawsuit**
**Active — Elevated Risk.** On April 27, 2026, Marvell Semiconductor (which acquired Celestial AI) cancelled all POET purchase orders, citing POET's breach of confidentiality obligations. The breach stemmed from a POET executive's April 21, 2026 YouTube/Stocktwits video interview in which they disclosed confidential details about the Celestial AI relationship, including invoice and shipping timelines. The stock fell 47.3% on the disclosure. Marvell has not publicly indicated any reinstatement of orders.

**(2) Securities Class Actions (PFIC Tax Misrepresentation)**
**Active — Multiple Suits Filed.** At least three securities class action lawsuits have been filed in the U.S. District Court for the District of New Jersey by firms including Rosen Law Firm and Block & Leviton. The class period spans approximately April 1–27, 2026. Allegations: (a) POET misrepresented or downplayed the risk of being classified as a Passive Foreign Investment Company (PFIC) — a designation that would impose significant tax burdens on U.S. shareholders; (b) the NDA breach and resulting order cancellation were foreseeable risks not disclosed to investors. Lead plaintiff deadline was June 29, 2026.

**(3) $400M Capital Raise — Dilution Impact**
**Completed — Ongoing Overhang.** In May 2026, POET raised $400M via a registered direct offering of ~19M shares at $21.00/share, with accompanying 3-year warrants at $26.25/share. At today's price (~$6.44), both the offering price and the warrants are deeply out of the money, and existing shareholders face severe dilution from the equity issuance. The total $830M raised over 12 months represents substantial dilutive pressure at current price levels. Additionally, up to $661M more may be raised via warrant exercises if the stock recovers above $26.25.

### Outlook: **BEARISH**
Despite an operational narrative of on-schedule production ramp and ambitious unit targets, POET faces a confluence of severe headwinds: its largest customer relationship (Marvell/Celestial AI) was severed and has not been reinstated, multiple securities class actions are in early litigation stages, and the stock trades at less than one-third of its most recent equity raise price ($21), with significant warrant-driven dilution potential if conditions recover. The absence of a named replacement customer makes near-term catalysts thin. Until POET announces a material new order or a positive resolution to the NDA litigation, the risk/reward skews negative.
